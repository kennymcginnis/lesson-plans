class: lesson contrast
title: A mistake is not your whole identity
---

<p class="eyebrow">A mistake is not your whole identity</p>

<p class="contrast-line">"I did something wrong."</p>

<p class="contrast-line fixed-label">"This is all I'll ever be."</p>

<p class="question">What is the difference?</p>

<!--
Continue 8-14 minutes. Follow students' observations instead of requiring an answer to every question.
Teaching point: Through Jesus Christ, cleansing is possible. Our mistakes need not determine who we become.
Forgiveness does not mean the past never happened or consequences disappear. Repairing harm, rebuilding trust, and healing can take time.
Continuing painful feelings are not proof that God has refused forgiveness.
-->

# Turning toward God.

<div class="actions">
<div><h2>Stop.</h2><p>What needs to end?</p></div>
<div><h2>Repair.</h2><p>What can be put right?</p></div>
<div><h2>Care.</h2><p>Who needs support?</p></div>
</div>

<p class="question">Do we have to succeed at changing<br>before asking God for help?</p>

<!--
Continue 14-21 minutes. These are application prompts, not quotations from the passage.
Possible examples if needed: stop joining the ridicule, acknowledge harm appropriately, include or support the person being mistreated.
Ask how this differs from trying to become good enough before asking God for help.
Keep both truths together: Christ offers cleansing, and repentance changes how we treat people.
Service is not payment for forgiveness. We can ask for His help as we begin changing.
If time runs short, take fewer reports and move to the closing at minute 21.
-->
