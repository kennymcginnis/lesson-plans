# The Lord Is There

<p class="subtitle">Where can hope begin when everything familiar is gone?</p>

<p v-click class="sample-answer"><strong>One possible answer:</strong> The Lord meets Ezekiel among the exiles and promises to give His people new life.</p>

# Glory in exile.

<p class="question">Where does Ezekiel see the Lord’s glory?</p>

<p v-click class="sample-answer"><strong>One possible answer:</strong> Among the exiles in Babylon, beside the river Chebar (Ezekiel 1:1–3, 28).</p>
