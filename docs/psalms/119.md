# Psalm 119 — A Path, Not Just a Lamp

## Scripture

> Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path. (119:105)

Not the whole road. Just the next step.

## A path, not just a lamp

As you read, look for: walk, path, way, feet, wander.

President Nelson: "If our journey through life is to be successful, we
need to follow divine direction... the Psalmist wrote, 'Thy word is a
lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.'"

Psalm 119 is 176 verses long and keeps returning to one idea: your life
is a journey back to Heavenly Father, and His word is what keeps you on
the path.

<details>
<summary>Teaching notes</summary>

You don't need to know your whole future to walk with Him — you need to
know your next right step, and scripture is what lights it.

Ask: "Where have you been, where are you now, where are you heading —
and what has God given you to help you stay on the path?"

Quick ask: "When did you last open the scriptures with an actual question
instead of just reading to finish?"

</details>
