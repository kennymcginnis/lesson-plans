# Psalm 107 — Then They Cried Unto the Lord

*Bonus — not part of this week's official reading, but fits the theme.*

## Scripture

> O give thanks unto the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth
> for ever. (107:1)
>
> Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble, and he delivered them
> out of their distresses. (107:6, echoed at 13, 19, 28)

## Four pictures of trouble, one way out

Lost, wandering in the wilderness. Bound in the darkness of a prison.
Sick because of your own transgression. Tossed in a storm at sea. Four
very different troubles — and every time, the pattern repeats: they
cried unto the Lord, and He delivered them.

> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his
> wonderful works to the children of men! (107:8, 15, 21, 31)

Each account closes with this same refrain.

**Teaching notes**

Ask: "Which of these four pictures — lost, bound, sick from your own
choices, or caught in a storm — feels closest to where you are right
now?" Every one of them ends the same way: cry unto the Lord.

> He satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with
> goodness. (107:9)

Elder Gerrit W. Gong footnoted this verse in "Eternity's Great Gifts:
Jesus Christ's Atonement, Resurrection, Restoration" (Apr. 2025).
