# Psalms 134–136 — No Idol Compares

## Scripture

> They have mouths, but they speak not: eyes have they, but they see not;
> They have ears, but they hear not: neither is there any breath in their
> mouths.
> They that make them are like unto them: so is every one that trusteth
> in them. (135:16–18)

## Make a list — like the psalmists did

Make a list — like the psalmists did — of things the Lord has actually
done for you.

<details>
<summary>Teaching notes</summary>

Why is it foolish to trust in a false god? Because it can't see, hear,
act, or save. Ask: "What might you be tempted to trust in that functions
like an idol — looks powerful but can't actually do anything for you?"

Simple activity, no props: everyone lists a few real things the Lord has
done for them this year, out loud or on paper.

</details>
