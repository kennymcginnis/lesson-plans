# Psalm 135 — No Idol Compares

## Scripture

> 16. They have mouths, but they speak not; eyes have they, but they see not;
> 17. They have ears, but they hear not; neither is there any breath in their mouths.
> 18. They that make them are like unto them: so is every one that trusteth in them. (135:16–18)

## Make a list — like the psalmists did

Make a list — like the psalmists did — of things the Lord has actually
done for you.

Why is it foolish to trust in a false god? Because it can't see, hear,
act, or save. 

### Q: What might you be tempted to trust in that functions like an idol </br>— looks powerful but can't actually do anything for you?"

Simple activity, no props: everyone lists a few real things the Lord has
done for them this year, out loud or on paper.
