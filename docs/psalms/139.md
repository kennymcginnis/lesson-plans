# Psalm 139 — The Lord Knows My Heart

## Scripture

> O Lord, thou hast searched me, and known me. (139:1)
>
> Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts. (139:23)

## Fully known, and still fully loved

Elder Ulisses Soares, "Seek Christ in Every Thought" (Oct. 2020), draws
on Psalm 139:1–3 to teach that God knows our thoughts, our path, and our
hearts completely.

<details>
<summary>Teaching notes</summary>

Ask: "How does it feel to know God already knows your thoughts and
actions, your strengths and weaknesses — and loves you anyway?"

</details>
