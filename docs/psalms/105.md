# Psalm 105 — Make Known His Deeds

## Scripture

> O give thanks unto the Lord, call upon his name:
> make known his deeds among the people. (105:1)

## Give thanks. Call on His name. Then go tell someone.

Praise doesn't stay private for long — it turns into testimony. This is
the verse you'd want to hand a missionary, or anyone heading out to
represent Christ: here's what to tell the world about Him.

<details>
<summary>Teaching notes</summary>

Ask: "Who taught you something about Jesus by telling you what He's done
for them?"

</details>
