# Psalms of Ascent — Psalms 120–134

## Scripture

> I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help. (121:1)

## Pilgrims sang these on the road up to Jerusalem

...to the temple, to the presence of God.

<details>
<summary>Teaching notes</summary>

The southern steps into the ancient temple were deliberately built uneven
— different widths — so pilgrims couldn't fall into a mindless rhythm.
Every step had to be deliberate.

Same idea elsewhere: a church on the Germany/Austria border with no
parking near it — you walk a mile to arrive, on purpose, so you have time
to prepare your heart.

</details>

## Warm up your heart before you pray

Martin Luther: "I have to warm up my heart for prayer."

The Psalms can be that warm-up — for gratitude, for love, for praise —
before you step into His presence.

This follows Psalm 118, the cornerstone psalm, and Psalm 128 in the
Psalms of Ascent. Before the climb toward His presence, you fix your
alignment to the cornerstone first.
