# Psalms 120–134 — Psalms of Ascent

## Scripture

> 1. I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help. (121:1)

## Pilgrims sang these on the road up to Jerusalem to the temple, to the presence of God.

The southern steps into the ancient temple were deliberately built uneven — different widths — so pilgrims couldn't fall
into a mindless rhythm. Every step had to be deliberate.

Same idea elsewhere: a church on the Germany/Austria border with no parking near it — you walk a mile to arrive, on
purpose, so you have time to prepare your heart.

## Warm up your heart before you pray

> "I have to warm up my heart for prayer." - Martin Luther

The Psalms can be that warm-up — for gratitude, for love, for praise — before you step into His presence.

This follows Psalm 118, the cornerstone psalm, and Psalm 128 in the Psalms of Ascent. Before the climb toward His
presence, you fix your alignment to the cornerstone first.

## Children are an heritage of the Lord

- **The Family: A Proclamation to the World**

> Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord... Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them. (127:3, 5)

This is where "children are an heritage of the Lord" in _The Family: A Proclamation to the World_ comes from.

> 1. Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it. (127:1)

### Exceeding Great and Precious Promises

- **President Howard W. Hunter** quoted this verse, along with "a 'heritage of the Lord'" (127:3), in his first address as
  President of the Church, "Exceeding Great and Precious Promises" (Oct. 1994). It was part of a broader appeal to
  protect the family as an eternal institution against the pressures pulling it apart.

  > Parenthood is a sacred obligation and privilege, with children welcomed as a "heritage of the Lord" (Ps. 127:3). ...
  >
  > 1. "Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it" (Ps. 127:1).

### Guidelines to Carry Forth the Work of God in Cleanliness

- **President Spencer W. Kimball** quoted "Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord... Happy is the man that hath his quiver
  full of them" (vv. 3 and 5) in "Guidelines to Carry Forth the Work of God in Cleanliness" (Apr. 1974). He used it to
  push back on the growing cultural trend of couples deliberately avoiding or delaying children, framing them instead as
  a reward, not a burden.
  > Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord: and the fruit of the womb is his reward. Happy is the man that hath his
  > quiver full of them.

### The Personal Journey of a Child of God

- **Elder Neil L. Andersen** later quoted the same verse in "The Personal Journey of a Child of God" (Apr. 2021), teaching
  that every one of God's spirit children arrives on their own personal journey and deserves to be welcomed,
  safeguarded, and loved.

  > The scriptures record that "children are an heritage of the Lord" (Psalm 127:3).

### And a Little Child Shall Lead Them

- **President Boyd K. Packer** quoted "happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them" in "And a Little Child Shall Lead
  Them" (Apr. 2012), a talk about how children's simplicity and innocence teach the adults around them what matters
  most.

### The Importance of the Family

- **Elder L. Tom Perry** quoted "Children are an heritage of the Lord" in at least three talks: "The Importance of the
  Family" (Apr. 2003), "Mothers Teaching Children in the Home" (Apr. 2010), and "Becoming Goodly Parents" (Oct. 2012) —
  he kept returning to it across his ministry as part of his consistent message that the family, not any institution
  outside it, is where God's work is centered.
  > Husband and wife have a solemn responsibility to love and care for each other and for their children. "Children are
  > an heritage of the Lord" (Psalm 127:3).

### Salvation and Exaltation

- **Elder Russell M. Nelson** cited the same verse in "Salvation and Exaltation" (Apr. 2008), teaching that exaltation is
  fundamentally a family matter — how a couple treats the children entrusted to them is part of their own eternal
  progression.
  > Our Heavenly Father wants husbands and wives to be faithful to each other and to esteem and treat their children as
  > an heritage from the Lord.

### To Become as One

- **President Henry B. Eyring** quoted it in "To Become as One," his address to the Vatican's international colloquium on
  marriage (Nov. 2014). He'd just told the story of his own 52-year marriage as evidence to an interfaith audience that
  marriage between a man and a woman has real power to build happiness, then quoted this line from the Family
  Proclamation to show what the Church teaches about the responsibility that comes with it.
  > Husband and wife have a solemn responsibility to love and care for each other and for their children. 3. "Children
  > are an heritage of the Lord" (Psalm 127:3).
