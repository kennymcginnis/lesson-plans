# Psalms of Ascent — Psalms 120–134

## Scripture

> I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help. (121:1)

## Pilgrims sang these on the road up to Jerusalem

...to the temple, to the presence of God.

<details>
<summary>Teaching notes</summary>

The southern steps into the ancient temple were deliberately built uneven
— different widths — so pilgrims couldn't fall into a mindless rhythm.
Every step had to be deliberate.

Same idea elsewhere: a church on the Germany/Austria border with no
parking near it — you walk a mile to arrive, on purpose, so you have time
to prepare your heart.

</details>

## Warm up your heart before you pray

Martin Luther: "I have to warm up my heart for prayer."

The Psalms can be that warm-up — for gratitude, for love, for praise —
before you step into His presence.

This follows Psalm 118, the cornerstone psalm, and Psalm 128 in the
Psalms of Ascent. Before the climb toward His presence, you fix your
alignment to the cornerstone first.

## He that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep

> Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep. (121:4)

President Gordon B. Hinckley quoted this verse repeatedly across his
ministry — as an Apostle in "He Slumbers Not, nor Sleeps" (Apr. 1983),
again in "God Is at the Helm" (Apr. 1994), and as President of the
Church in "Till We Meet Again" (Oct. 2001).

## Children are an heritage of the Lord

> Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord... Happy is the man that hath
> his quiver full of them. (127:3, 5)

This is where "children are an heritage of the Lord" in *The Family: A
Proclamation to the World* comes from.

> Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it. (127:1)

President Howard W. Hunter quoted this verse, along with "a 'heritage
of the Lord'" (127:3), in his first address as President of the
Church, "Exceeding Great and Precious Promises" (Oct. 1994).

President Spencer W. Kimball quoted "Lo, children are an heritage of
the Lord... Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them" (127:3,
5) in "Guidelines to Carry Forth the Work of God in Cleanliness" (Apr.
1974). Elder Neil L. Andersen later quoted the same verse in "The
Personal Journey of a Child of God" (Apr. 2021), and President Boyd K.
Packer quoted "happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them" in
"And a Little Child Shall Lead Them" (Apr. 2012). Elder L. Tom Perry
quoted "Children are an heritage of the Lord" in at least three talks:
"The Importance of the Family" (Apr. 2003), "Mothers Teaching Children
in the Home" (Apr. 2010), and "Becoming Goodly Parents" (Oct. 2012).
Elder Russell M. Nelson cited the same verse in "Salvation and
Exaltation" (Apr. 2008). President Henry B. Eyring, then a counselor in
the First Presidency, quoted it in "To Become as One," his address to
the Vatican's international colloquium on marriage (Nov. 2014).
