# Psalm 128 — Happy Are We

## Scripture

> 1. Blessed is every one that feareth the Lord; that walketh in his ways.
> 2. For thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and
> it shall be well with thee. (128:1–2)

## Walking with Him doesn't just make you obedient. It makes you happy.

Real happiness — the kind you can see on someone's face — comes from the
ordinary, daily choice to walk in His ways, not just from big spiritual
highs.
