# Psalm 116 — I Love the Lord Because...

## Scripture

> I love the Lord, because he hath heard my voice and my supplications. (116:1)
>
> For thou hast delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, and my
> feet from falling. (116:8)

## Writing a psalm is as simple as four words

"I love the Lord" — followed by "because," and then your list.

Give the class 60 seconds, quietly, to write their own list: "I love the
Lord because ___." No sharing required unless someone wants to. This is
the whole activity — a pencil and a piece of paper, nothing staged.

## Addicted to appreciation

Not "I'll be grateful when something big happens." Grateful for legs. For
an alarm clock. For eggs for breakfast. For a window to look out of.

**I love the Lord because ___.**

The small, ordinary things count. If you want a praiseworthy day, write
those four words and fill in the blank — today, and see what you notice.

## Precious in the sight of the Lord

> Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints. (116:15)

President Russell M. Nelson quoted this verse in "Decisions for
Eternity" (Oct. 2013), teaching that death is precious because it is a
"homecoming" for the Saint. He'd quoted the same verse in "Doors of
Death" (Apr. 1992) and "We Are Children of God" (Oct. 1998). Elder
Bruce R. McConkie also quoted it in "The Dead Who Die in the Lord"
(Oct. 1976).

## What shall I render unto the Lord?

Elder Dieter F. Uchtdorf, "O How Great the Plan of Our God!" (Oct.
2016), points to Psalm 116:12: "What shall I render unto the Lord for
all his benefits toward me?" — the same question this whole psalm is
answering.
