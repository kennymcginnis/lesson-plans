# Psalm 102–103 — When Life Feels Heavy

## Scripture

**Psalm 102 (selected)**

> Hear my prayer, O Lord, and let my cry come unto thee...
> My days are consumed like smoke...
> I am like a pelican in the wilderness, an owl in the desert...
> I am withered like grass.

**Psalm 103:1–5**

> Bless the Lord, O my soul... and forget not all his benefits:
> who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases;
> who redeemeth thy life from destruction; who crowneth thee with
> lovingkindness and tender mercies; who satisfieth thy mouth with
> good things.

## That's not exactly praise. And that's the point.

Read enough of Psalm 102 out loud that the weight of it lands. This psalm
gives ancient words to very familiar feelings — anxiety, depression,
exhaustion. It ends still begging God to hear. Don't rush past this. Let
it feel heavy for a moment.

## One psalm away

Right after the desperate prayer of Psalm 102 comes Psalm 103:

> Bless the Lord, O my soul... forget not all his benefits. (103:1–2)

On days like 102, it's easy to forget what God is capable of. Psalm 103
is the reminder — and it's only one psalm away. If you're living in a 102
kind of week, you are not far from being reminded who God is.

Mark the word "all" in verse 3 — that's not an exaggeration, it's the text.

**You were eating ashes one chapter ago. Now your mouth is satisfied with
good things.**
