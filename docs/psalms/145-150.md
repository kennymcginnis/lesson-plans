# Psalms 145–150 — Let Everything That Hath Breath Praise the Lord

## Scripture

> Every day will I bless thee, and I will praise thy name for ever and
> ever. (145:2)
>
> Happy is he... whose hope is in the Lord his God. (146:5)
>
> Let every thing that hath breath praise the Lord. (150:6)

## That's a bold thing to ask for

Everything that has breath, every day, forever. Who is worthy of that
much praise, from that many people, for that long?

## The Lord is good to all

> His tender mercies are over all his works.
> Thou satisfiest the desire of every living thing.
> The Lord is nigh unto all them. (145:9, 16, 18)

<details>
<summary>Teaching notes</summary>

Your own story goes here: a time you found God's goodness somewhere you
didn't expect it — a remote village, a hard season, an ordinary Tuesday.
Keep it to 90 seconds, specific, no moral tacked on at the end. The
point: anywhere you go, you can find His goodness there.

</details>

## Who is worthy of that much praise?

> He gathereth together the outcasts...
> He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds...
> He telleth the number of the stars; he calleth them all by their
> names...
> Great is our Lord, and of great power: his understanding is infinite. (147:2–5)

Every person alive has, at some point, experienced one of these: felt
like an outcast, felt broken, needed binding up, needed to be known and
called by name.

<details>
<summary>Teaching notes</summary>

Simple daily practice, no props: tonight, look back over today and try to
find one of these — a moment you felt welcomed, healed, known, or seen.
That's the answer to "who is worthy."

</details>
