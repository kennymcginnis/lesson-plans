# Closing - A Faithful Next Step

## Small means, real trust

Nephi makes a bow, asks for direction, prepares tools, works, and prays. His story joins a willing response with reliance on the Lord. It does not make him immune to grief or harm.

[Read 1 Nephi 16:23-29](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/16?lang=eng&id=p23-p29#p23)

## Questions to sit with

- What is one part of a present difficulty that is within your influence?
- What help do you need from God or another person?
- Which small practice could help you keep moving toward Christ?

## One response for the week

Choose one action and one request for help. Make a repair, prepare for a task, resume prayer, or ask someone for counsel. The next step does not need to solve everything to matter.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. His guidance can sustain us through a journey whose whole course we do not yet understand.
