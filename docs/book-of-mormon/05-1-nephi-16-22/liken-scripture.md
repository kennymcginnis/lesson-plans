# 1 Nephi 19-22 - Liken Scripture to Learn of Christ

## Why Nephi reads to his family

Chapter 19 explains Nephi's record-keeping and bears witness of the Redeemer's suffering and patience. Nephi then explains why he reads scripture to his people:

> 23. "I did liken all scriptures unto us, that it might be for our profit and learning."

1 Nephi 19:23, excerpt.

[Read 1 Nephi 19:7-24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/19?lang=eng&id=p7-p24#p7)

The stated purpose is to persuade them to believe in the Lord their Redeemer. Application should move us toward Christ, not simply turn every passage into a story about our own preferences.

## Isaiah and the covenant

Chapters 20-21 reproduce Isaiah 48-49. Read the invitation to leave Babylon, the servant's work, and the promise that Zion is not forgotten. These collective covenant promises can nourish personal trust without guaranteeing a particular outcome for every reader.

[Read 1 Nephi 20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/20?lang=eng)

[Read 1 Nephi 21:14-16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/21?lang=eng&id=p14-p16#p14)

In chapter 22, Nephi answers his brothers' questions about the meaning of what they have heard. His explanation concerns scattering, gathering, God's covenants, and the Holy One of Israel. The lesson does not end merely with a successful voyage; it widens toward God's purposes for His children.

[Read 1 Nephi 22:1-12, 25-31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/22?lang=eng&id=p1-p12,p25-p31#p1)

## Try a careful application

First identify who is addressed and what the passage says. Then ask what it reveals about God and what response fits His teachings. For example, a promise that Zion is remembered can invite us to bring feelings of abandonment to the Lord. It does not mean we know exactly when every loss will be repaired.

## Questions to sit with

- Why does Nephi say he reads scripture to his family?
- What is the difference between likening a passage and ignoring its setting?
- How do the promises about Israel connect the family's story with a larger work?
- What have you learned about Christ that you could act on this week?
