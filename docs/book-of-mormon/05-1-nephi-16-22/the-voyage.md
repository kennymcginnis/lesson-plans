# 1 Nephi 18 - Prayer on the Journey

## Repeated prayer and practical work

Nephi describes working the timbers as the Lord showed him and going often to the mountain to pray. The ship is completed through sustained work and instruction, not a single moment of inspiration.

[Read 1 Nephi 18:1-8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/18?lang=eng&id=p1-p8#p1)

After the household boards the ship, the sea journey brings another crisis. Some forget by whose power they have come so far. Nephi's warning is met with violence, and he is bound. The compass ceases to work, and a storm threatens the family.

[Read 1 Nephi 18:9-21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/18?lang=eng&id=p9-p21#p9)

The account condemns their rudeness, rebellion, and mistreatment; it should not be simplified into a rule that singing or dancing is inherently wrong. Notice also the distress of parents, children, and Nephi's wife. Harm spreads beyond the immediate conflict.

## Praise without denying pain

> 16. "Nevertheless, I did look unto my God, and I did praise him all the day long."

1 Nephi 18:16, excerpt.

Nephi also describes swollen wrists and ankles and great soreness. His gratitude does not erase the harm. Once released, he prays, the storm ceases, and they resume the journey.

This particular deliverance invites trust; it does not guarantee every storm will end on our timetable or require a person to remain in an abusive situation. Praise, lament, seeking safety, and receiving care need not be opposites.

## A beginning in a new land

Verses 23-25 describe arrival, planting, and finding resources. The promised land still involves work. The family's need to choose faithfulness does not end at the shore.

[Read 1 Nephi 18:22-25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/18?lang=eng&id=p22-p25#p22)

## Questions to sit with

- How do repeated prayer and ordinary labor belong together in this account?
- What happens when a group forgets the help that brought it forward?
- How can gratitude be honest without denying pain or excusing wrongdoing?
- What will help you continue a good practice after an immediate crisis has passed?
