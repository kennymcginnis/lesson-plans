# By Small Means

Week 5 - 1 Nephi 16-22

> When something important breaks, what can we do, and what help can we seek from the Lord?

## Lesson notes

Nephi's family receives guidance but still experiences hunger, bereavement, disagreement, and danger. Nephi makes a new bow, asks where to find ore for tools, prays repeatedly, and turns toward God during the voyage. His response joins action with dependence on the Lord.

The narrative leads into Nephi's purpose in keeping records and using scripture to persuade his family to believe in their Redeemer. The journey matters, but so does what its readers learn about Jesus Christ.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Read the problem, 4 minutes:** Read 1 Nephi 16:18-23. Name the family's hunger and Nephi's response without minimizing their distress.
2. **Small means and real action, 8 minutes:** Read 1 Nephi 16:24-29 and 17:8-10. Ask what lies within our control and how we can include the Lord.
3. **Trust on the journey, 7 minutes:** Read 1 Nephi 18:15-21. Discuss prayer and gratitude without suggesting that faithful people should remain in danger or never grieve.
4. **Liken to learn of Christ, 6 minutes:** Read 1 Nephi 19:23. Choose one thoughtful application and close with testimony.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize 1 Nephi 16:23-29; 17:8-10, 45; 18:16, 21; and 19:23. Read chapters 20-22 alongside Nephi's stated purpose: to help people trust the Redeemer and understand God's covenant work.
