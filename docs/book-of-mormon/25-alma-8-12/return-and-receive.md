# Alma 8 - Return and Receive Help

## The city does not respond as Alma hopes

Alma works in Ammonihah and wrestles with God in prayer for the people. They reject him and cast him out. He leaves weighed down with sorrow before an angel tells him to return.

[Read Alma 8:8-18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/8?lang=eng&id=p8-p18#p8)

Notice the angel's encouragement:

> 15. "Thou hast been faithful in keeping the commandments of God."

Alma 8:15, excerpt.

The affirmation comes before the city is converted. Faithful service is not measured only by immediate results or another person's response. Alma's discouragement does not erase what he has offered.

## Practical care is part of the answer

Alma returns speedily and asks a man for food. Amulek welcomes him because an angel has prepared him. The account joins divine direction with something ordinary and necessary: a meal and a place to stay.

[Read Alma 8:19-32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/8?lang=eng&id=p19-p32#p19)

Verse 27 says Alma remains with Amulek many days before preaching. There is room for companionship and preparation, not only immediate public action. The text does not describe every activity during that stay.

For us, receiving help may be part of continuing faithfully. A meal, rest, wise counsel, or collaboration can make a task sustainable. We should not dismiss practical needs because a work is spiritual.

## A specific return, not a universal command

Alma receives a particular commission to return. The story is not permission to insist that someone go back into an abusive or unsafe situation or repeatedly pressure a person who has declined contact. Discernment, boundaries, and respect for agency still matter in our applications.

Ask what the Lord has actually asked of us, what is within our responsibility, and what support we need. Perseverance can include changing methods rather than endlessly repeating an unsuccessful approach.

## Questions to sit with

- What does the angel praise before any visible success is reported?
- How do Alma and Amulek each receive an opportunity to serve?
- What kind of practical help could support a discouraged person?
- How can we continue faithfully without making another person's choices our personal scorecard?
