# Return, Remember, and Receive

Week 25 - Alma 8-12

> What if a difficult response does not mean that our faithful effort has been wasted?

## Lesson notes

Alma prays and works among people who reject his message. An angel affirms his faithfulness and sends him back. Amulek provides food, a place to stay, and eventually a second witness. Their teaching calls people to remember God's dealings and receive redemption through Christ.

The lesson follows encouragement, partnership, remembrance, and a changed response. It does not promise that every return will be welcomed or that another person's belief can be secured by our effort alone.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Faithful despite rejection, 4 minutes:** Read Alma 8:10-18. Ask what the angel recognizes before the city's response changes.
2. **Help and a turning point, 8 minutes:** Read Alma 8:19-27 and 10:5-8. Notice practical care and Amulek's decision to hear and act.
3. **Remember the Redeemer, 7 minutes:** Read Alma 9:20-23 and 11:37, 42-44. Discuss God's past help and Christ's promise of resurrection and redemption.
4. **Choose to receive, 6 minutes:** Read Alma 12:9-10, 24. Invite one private response of listening, repentance, or service and close with testimony.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize Alma 8:15-27; 9:20-23; 10:5-8, 22-23; 11:37, 42-44; and 12:9-10, 24, 33-34.

## How the modern teachings connect

These studies are grounded in scripture. Amulek's date is given in the Nephite calendar, not identified as July 4. Alma's stay with Amulek provides space for preparation; the text does not specifically say he took a nap. A proposed quotation attributed to Winston Churchill about a special moment of opportunity has not been verified and is not used as an established source.
