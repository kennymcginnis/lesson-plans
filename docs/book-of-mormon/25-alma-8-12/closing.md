# Closing - Receive the Next Invitation

## Faithfulness can begin again

Alma receives encouragement after rejection. Amulek changes his response after earlier resistance. Together they teach about remembering God and receiving redemption through Christ.

[Read Alma 8:15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/8?lang=eng&id=p15#p15)

[Read Alma 10:6-8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/10?lang=eng&id=p6-p8#p6)

## Questions to sit with

- Do you most need encouragement to continue, help from another person, or willingness to listen?
- What part of God's past help would you like to remember?
- What could you do with the next invitation you already understand?

## One response for the week

Offer practical care, accept support, record a memory of God's help, or respond to a clear call to repent or serve. Keep the choice specific and respectful of other people's agency.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. We can trust His work without needing to see every result of our prayers today.
