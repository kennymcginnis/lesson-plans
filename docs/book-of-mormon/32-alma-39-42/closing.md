# Closing - Let Mercy Have Claim

## The conversation does not end with rejection

Alma has spoken plainly about wrongdoing, answered questions, and taught about God's justice and mercy. His final words call Corianton to preach so that others may receive the great plan of mercy.

[Read Alma 42:29-31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/42?lang=eng&id=p29-p31#p29)

The future is not a denial of the past. It includes repentance, change, and service made possible through Jesus Christ.

## Questions to sit with

- What has this conversation taught you about God's intent?
- Is there a truth you need to receive without making it a verdict against your whole worth?
- What next step could express both responsibility and trust in mercy?

## One response for the week

Seek appropriate counsel, make a needed repair, strengthen a helpful boundary, or offer service with greater honesty and care. Keep personal repentance private and respect the wishes and safety of anyone who was harmed.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. His plan invites us home through real redemption, not through pretending we never needed Him.
