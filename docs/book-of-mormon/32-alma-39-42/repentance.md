# Alma 39 - Correction with a Future

## A serious conversation

Alma names Corianton's boasting, abandonment of his ministry, and pursuit of Isabel. He explains that Corianton's conduct affected how others received his teaching. This is more than a private embarrassment; other people and a position of trust are involved.

[Read Alma 39:1-13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/39?lang=eng&id=p1-p13#p1)

The language of verse 5 is severe. Read it in the setting of Alma's whole account, not as a label assigning equal guilt to every sexual situation. Someone who has been assaulted or coerced is not guilty of another person's wrongdoing. Do not invite graphic discussion or use the passage to stigmatize people.

## I would not dwell upon your crimes, except for your good

Verse 7 explains the purpose of the painful conversation: Corianton's good. Alma goes on to give direction for a different response. Naming a wrong is not the same as declaring that the person can never change.

The counsel includes humility, attending to instruction, governing desires, and not being led away by wealth or vain things. The wording in verse 9 concerns repentance and self-restraint; do not replace it with a more comfortable phrase and then call that phrase scripture.

## Return and acknowledge

> "Return unto them, and acknowledge your faults and that wrong which ye have done."

Alma 39:13, excerpt.

Accountability includes more than privately feeling bad. It can require honest acknowledgment, repair where possible, changed behavior, and appropriate help. Contact with a person who was harmed should respect their safety and wishes; repentance does not entitle us to access, trust, or immediate reconciliation.

As a teaching application, practical boundaries can help us avoid predictable temptations. Humility means admitting that good intentions alone do not make us immune. It does not mean treating every person as untrustworthy or using fear to control a child.

## The message returns to Christ

Verses 15-19 explain the coming of Christ and the good news of salvation. Alma wants Corianton to understand why the message matters and why his conduct in carrying it matters.

[Read Alma 39:15-19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/39?lang=eng&id=p15-p19#p15)

The later conclusion in Alma 42:31 renews the call to preach. The entire conversation leaves room for a future of faithful service, not merely a permanent identity as someone who failed.

## Questions to sit with

- How does Alma explain why he is addressing the wrongdoing?
- What practical counsel points toward a different future?
- What is the difference between accountability and endless humiliation?
- What help, boundary, or private act of repentance could support a needed change?
