# Listen to the Witnesses

## Compare what the statements report

Read both testimonies. Rather than assuming they describe identical experiences, identify what each actually says.

| Statement | People named | What they report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three Witnesses | Oliver Cowdery, David Whitmer, Martin Harris | An angel showed the plates; they saw the engravings and heard divine testimony concerning the translation. |
| Eight Witnesses | Christian, Jacob, Peter Jr., and John Whitmer; Hiram Page; Joseph Sr., Hyrum, and Samuel H. Smith | Joseph Smith showed the plates; they handled leaves, saw engravings, and hefted the plates. |

[Read the Testimony of Three Witnesses](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/three?lang=eng)

[Read the Testimony of Eight Witnesses](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/eight?lang=eng)

The Three describe their experience as a gift of God's grace and a command to bear record. The Eight describe handling and seeing. Neither statement claims that the witnesses personally translated the record.

## A witness accepts responsibility

Both testimonies address the world. The Three also speak of remaining faithful in Christ. Their report of an experience does not remove their responsibility for subsequent choices.

Listen to the published statements before adding later recollections of conversations or final testimonies. Such accounts have their own authors, dates, and evidentiary limits. No unverified later recollection is needed to state what these signed testimonies claim.

## Joseph Smith's testimony

Joseph's account describes Moroni's visit, the message about a work God had for him, the record, and the means prepared for its translation. Notice fear giving way to attention, repeated instruction, and time spent considering what he had heard.

[Read the Testimony of the Prophet Joseph Smith](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/js?lang=eng)

This is the book's account of its coming forth. We can study it thoughtfully without claiming that our own spiritual experiences must reproduce Joseph's or the witnesses'. The invitation to seek a personal witness is not a promise of an identical vision or physical encounter.

## Questions to sit with

- What similarities and differences do you notice between the Three and Eight Witnesses?
- Why is it useful to separate what a witness reports from conclusions we add?
- What responsibility comes with sharing what we sincerely believe we have learned?
- How can we bear testimony honestly without pretending to know more than we do?
