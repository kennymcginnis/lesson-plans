# The Title Page - Who, How, and Why?

## Read the book's stated purpose

The title page introduces an abridged record, its preservation and translation, its audience, and its purposes. Begin by reading it in full rather than skipping directly to the story.

[Read the title page](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/bofm-title?lang=eng)

## Three questions to mark

| Question | Look for | Discuss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| For whom? | The remnant of the house of Israel, Jew, and Gentile | How wide is the invitation to read? |
| How? | Abridgment, preservation, revelation, and the gift and power of God | What kind of record does the book claim to be? |
| Why? | Remembering God's dealings, knowing His covenants, and being convinced that Jesus is the Christ | How could these purposes guide our study? |

Work individually or give one question to each small group. Ask for the actual phrase that supports each answer. The activity requires only the text.

## Not cast off forever

The title page joins remembering God's past dealings with knowing that His covenant people are not cast off forever. The book's witness of Jesus Christ belongs beside that assurance of God's faithfulness.

> "Jesus is the Christ, the Eternal God, manifesting himself unto all nations"

Title page, excerpt.

The audience includes descendants of the peoples described in the record, but its purpose reaches the nations. Do not turn covenant language into a ranking of present-day peoples or a claim that some readers matter less to God.

## A sacred purpose and human records

The title page acknowledges the possibility of human mistakes. That qualification does not erase the book's claim to divine purpose; it gives us reason to read carefully without demanding that every historical or textual question be settled before we can learn from it.

The introduction, witness statements, and brief explanation serve different purposes from the translated title page. Keeping those distinctions clear helps us ask appropriate questions of each text.

## Questions to sit with

- Which purpose would you like to keep in view as you read this year?
- What might you notice if you read looking for God's covenants as well as individual stories?
- How does the title page direct attention toward Jesus Christ?
- What question would you like your study to help you explore?
