# Study to Come Nearer to God

## Abide by its precepts

The introduction identifies the Savior's ministry among the Nephites as the book's crowning event. It also quotes Joseph Smith's teaching that a person can get nearer to God by abiding by the book's precepts.

[Read the introduction](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/introduction?lang=eng)

Notice the action: abiding, not simply possessing or finishing. Nearness to God can include trust, changed conduct, and a willingness to receive His help. Learning information matters, but conversion reaches the way we live.

## Eight ways to engage with scripture

| Practice | Companion passage | Try it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meditate | Joshua 1:8 | Return to a phrase during the day. |
| Study | D&C 11:22 | Examine the setting and surrounding verses. |
| Treasure up | D&C 84:85 | Remember words that can guide a future choice. |
| Liken | 1 Nephi 19:23 | Identify an application without rewriting the original context. |
| Feast | 2 Nephi 32:3 | Give a passage sustained attention. |
| Search | Mosiah 1:7 | Follow a question through related passages. |
| Lay hold | Helaman 3:29 | Act on a truth rather than merely admire it. |
| Ponder | 3 Nephi 17:3 | Reflect, pray for understanding, and return. |

[Read 3 Nephi 17:3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/17?lang=eng&id=p3#p3)

The point is not to stop reading. It is to make reading part of a fuller response to God's word.

## Russell M. Nelson - The Book of Mormon: What Would Your Life Be Like without It?

**Verified teaching about prayerful study.** President Nelson, then President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, teaches:

> "The truths of the Book of Mormon have the power to heal, comfort, restore, succor, strengthen, console, and cheer our souls."

He invites daily prayerful study and pondering, with promises of direction and help in making decisions. This is not a guarantee that every question will be answered immediately or that reading removes the need for other appropriate help.

[October 2017 address](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2017/10/the-book-of-mormon-what-would-your-life-be-like-without-it?lang=eng)

## Read, remember, ponder, and ask

Moroni 10:3-5 invites remembrance of God's mercy, pondering, and asking with sincerity, real intent, and faith in Christ. Read the whole invitation rather than turning it into a timed test of whether a particular feeling appears.

[Read Moroni 10:3-5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/moro/10?lang=eng&id=p3-p5#p3)

Set a manageable time and place to study. A reminder, reading plan, or notebook can help. Adapt the format to your needs, including audio or accessible text. Missing a day is a reason to return, not to abandon the practice or measure your worth by a streak.

## Questions to sit with

- Which study verb would make the biggest difference to your current reading?
- What is one precept you could practice instead of only underline?
- What helps you ask sincere questions while being willing to act on an answer?
- What realistic study pattern could you begin this week?
