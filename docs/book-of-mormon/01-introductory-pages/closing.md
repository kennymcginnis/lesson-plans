# Closing - Begin with Christ

## A purpose for the year

The book invites us to remember God's dealings, understand His covenants, and come to know Jesus Christ. Its witnesses offer their testimony; each reader is invited to study, ponder, pray, and live what they learn.

[Return to the title page](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/bofm-title?lang=eng)

The brief explanation of the Book of Mormon helps distinguish its major records and abridgments. Use it when the book's structure becomes confusing rather than assuming every chapter has the same narrator or setting.

[Read A Brief Explanation about the Book of Mormon](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/explanation?lang=eng)

## Questions to sit with

- What do you hope to learn about Jesus Christ through this study?
- Which practice will help you read with more attention and less hurry?
- What is one response you can make to a truth you already recognize?

## One response for the week

Choose a short passage, one sincere question, and one action. Make room for a gradual deepening of understanding rather than demanding an identical experience to someone else's.

Close with your own testimony. Speak honestly about what you know and what you are still learning, and allow others room to do the same.
