# Jacob 6-7 - Cleave unto God and Witness of Christ

## Jacob explains the response

After the allegory, Jacob speaks of God's remembrance of Israel, roots and branches. Then he asks his listeners to respond:

> "Cleave unto God as he cleaveth unto you."

Jacob 6:5, excerpt.

[Read Jacob 6:1-5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/6?lang=eng&id=p1-p5#p1)

Repentance is not only fear of what might be lost. It is turning toward the God who remembers, reaches, and invites. The allegory becomes a question about the heart: will we receive His care or resist it?

Jacob's warnings in the rest of the chapter take rejection seriously. His invitation should not become a public judgment about whose heart is hard; begin with our own willingness to listen and change.

## Sherem and the witness of Christ

Jacob 7 records Sherem's opposition to Jacob's teaching of Christ. Jacob responds with his own knowledge, scripture, and the witness of the Holy Ghost. He says the scriptures truly testify of Christ.

[Read Jacob 7:1-12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/7?lang=eng&id=p1-p12#p1)

The later sign, confession, and death are part of a sobering account. They are not an instruction to seek punishment for someone who disagrees or a reason to label every honest question as Sherem's deception. Do not make threats or demand a miraculous sign as proof of another person's faith.

## Peace and renewed searching

Verse 23 describes peace, God's love, and searching the scriptures after the crisis. Jacob's closing words also express sorrow over his people's wandering and conflict. He hands responsibility to Enos rather than pretending the whole future is under his control.

[Read Jacob 7:23-27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/7?lang=eng&id=p23-p27#p23)

The goal of faithful witness is not winning an argument. It is helping people come to Christ, search His word, and live in His love.

## Questions to sit with

- What does it mean that God cleaves to us before Jacob asks us to cleave to Him?
- How can repentance express trust rather than only fear?
- What helps a testimony remain clear without becoming hostile toward questions?
- How could scripture study strengthen peace in a relationship or community?
