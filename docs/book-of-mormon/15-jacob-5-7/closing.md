# Closing - Receive His Care, Join His Work

## The Lord is still laboring

The vineyard's Lord remembers, nourishes, grieves, and works with His servants. Jacob asks us to respond with a full purpose of heart and faith in Christ.

[Read Jacob 5:72-75](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/5?lang=eng&id=p72-p75#p72)

[Read Jacob 6:4-5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/6?lang=eng&id=p4-p5#p4)

## Questions to sit with

- What does the Lord's persistence help you trust about His character?
- Where might you need to receive help instead of assuming you must grow alone?
- What part of His work can you join without trying to control someone else's response?

## One response for the week

Choose one act of receiving and one act of giving: seek counsel or return to prayer, then offer a respectful invitation or practical help. Remember that the Lord labors with His servants.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. Our hope rests in His mercy and our willing response, not in an outward comparison of whose life looks like the best soil.
