## Finding specific passages

- The women's song about Saul and David is in [1 Samuel 18:7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-sam/18?lang=eng&id=p7#p7).
- David's order to arm his men is in [1 Samuel 25:13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-sam/25?lang=eng&id=p13#p13).
- Jonathan's love for David and their covenant are described in [1 Samuel 18:1-3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-sam/18?lang=eng&id=p1-p3#p1), specifically verses 1 and 3.
- Bartimaeus's plea, "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me," is in [Mark 10:47](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/10?lang=eng&id=p47#p47).

## Translations and interpretation

Scripture excerpts identify their translation, including KJV, NIV, NRSVue, and NKJV. Linked Church scripture pages generally display KJV wording, which may differ from a quoted modern translation. Exact modern-translation wording has not been independently checked against every publisher's edition. Square brackets identify supplied words, and ellipses indicate omissions.

The comparisons between David's relationships and Jesus Christ are thematic readings. They do not imply that every event is a direct prophecy of Christ or that David's conduct always represents the Savior's character.

Jonathan's gifts in 1 Samuel 18:4 can be interpreted as expressions of loyalty and a willingness to yield status. The passage does not record a speech surrendering all power; Jonathan's explicit recognition of David's future kingship appears in 1 Samuel 23:17.

## Modern teachings

### Gordon B. Hinckley - Verified excerpt

The quotation beginning "So many of us make a great fuss of matters of small consequence" matches the official text of ["Slow to Anger," October 2007](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2007/10/slow-to-anger?lang=eng). It is a thematic pairing with David and Michal, not presented as a direct scripture citation from that address.

## Quotations awaiting verification

The following quotations are retained for further research, not used as verified authorities in the teaching pages.

### Leadership transition - Speaker and source unidentified

An account of a leadership transition includes this exchange:

> "[When one of his colleagues on the transition team asked him] 'President, are you scared?' [he responded,] 'If I thought we had to execute this transition relying exclusively upon our own experience and our own judgment, then I would be terrified. But we will have help from heaven. Because we know who is in charge and that we are not alone, then no, I am not scared.'"

The speaker, date, and publication have not been identified. Do not assign the words to a particular president or present them as a verified quotation from a Church publication.

### Quentin L. Cook - Attribution recorded, article unverified

The following statement is attributed to Elder Quentin L. Cook:

> "Because of [Christ's] Atonement, all that is unfair about this life is resolved."

The attribution lists Church News, September 11, 2025, but no article title or URL has been established. The wording, attribution, and original context remain unverified.

### Timothy Keller - Attribution recorded, original source unverified

The following passage is attributed to Timothy Keller:

> "You have to do exactly what Jonathan did. You have to look at God's anointed, and you have to say...'If [you are] going to get in my life, I have to get off the throne of my life. I can't just believe in you. I can't just try to emulate you. I have to give you my life. I have to take off my robe...' I have to say, 'You're first. You're absolute Lord of my life.'"

No sermon or publication has been identified. Keller is a Christian author outside The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The exact original wording and context remain unverified.
