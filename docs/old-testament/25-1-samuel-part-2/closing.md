# Closing - Every Heart Has a Throne

> Every heart has a throne.
> Who sits on yours?
> You, or the Son of David?

## Return to the relationships

- **David and Goliath:** Remember the Lord's help and trust Him in the struggle.
- **David and Michal:** Examine the words we use when hurt, embarrassed, or offended.
- **David and Abigail:** Receive wise correction and make room for someone to interrupt a harmful course.
- **David and Jonathan:** Help others find strength in God and reconsider the desire to be first.
- **The Son of David:** Seek the Savior's mercy and give Him first place in our lives.

David is not a flawless model in every episode. The lesson can lead us to Jesus both through examples of faith and through the failures that show our need for His grace.

## Questions to sit with

- Which relationship in the lesson helped you recognize something in your own life?
- Where might pride, fear, or resentment be competing with trust in Christ?
- What is one choice through which you could give the Savior first place this week?

Allow a private reflection. There is no need to ask class members to describe a family conflict or personal sin publicly.

## One response for the week

Choose a small, specific response: remember an instance of God's help, change one sentence you tend to say in anger, receive advice without defensiveness, or help someone else succeed.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. The throne image invites a lived response, not merely an answer to a question about the past.
