# Psalm 105 — Make Known His Deeds

## Scripture

> 1. O give thanks unto the Lord, call upon his name: make known his deeds among the people. (105:1)

## Give thanks. Call on His name. Then go tell someone.

Praise doesn't stay private for long — it turns into testimony.

If you were sending someone out as a missionary, or to represent Christ in any way, this is the verse you'd hand them — it tells them exactly what to say about Him.

### Q: "Who has taught you something about Jesus just by telling you what He's done in their life?"
