# Genesis 5; Moses 6 - Remember, Teach, and Be Born Again

## A life remembered as walking with God

Genesis 5 names generations, children, years of life, and death. Within that repeated pattern, Enoch's account stands out:

> 24. "And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him."

Genesis 5:24, KJV.

[Read Genesis 5:21-24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/5?lang=eng&id=p21-p24#p21)

Genesis is brief; Moses 6 gives more of Enoch's call and preaching. Read them together without forcing the genealogical ages into an explanation the lesson does not establish. The spiritual question is what it means for a life to be characterized by walking with God.

## A book of remembrance

Moses 6:5-6 describes inspired writing, a book of remembrance, and children learning to read and write. Faith is taught and preserved across generations rather than left only to isolated experiences.

[Read Moses 6:5-9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/6?lang=eng&id=p5-p9#p5)

Keeping a brief record of an answered prayer, a question, a family story, or a lesson learned can help us remember. Teaching can happen through ordinary words and example. People without children at home can also strengthen faith through friendships, mentoring, and congregational service.

## What Enoch teaches about Christ

Enoch's message includes the Lord's teaching to Adam about repentance, baptism, the Holy Ghost, and salvation through Jesus Christ. His story is not just about becoming a more impressive speaker; it concerns what people need to hear about their Redeemer.

[Read Moses 6:50-62](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/6?lang=eng&id=p50-p62#p50)

Verse 54 says that parents' sins are not answered upon their children's heads. Read this before interpreting the difficult language about conception and sin in verse 55. Do not use the passage to teach that babies are morally guilty or that a child inherits a parent's personal blame.

## Born again through the Savior

Verse 59 connects the imagery of physical birth with spiritual rebirth through water, the Spirit, and the blood of the Only Begotten. Verse 60 states:

> 60. "For by the water ye keep the commandment; by the Spirit ye are justified, and by the blood ye are sanctified;"

Moses 6:60.

[Read Moses 6:59-68](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/6?lang=eng&id=p59-p68#p59)

The account describes Adam's baptism and being born of the Spirit. It points to God's saving work, not merely our effort at self-improvement. Walking with Him includes repentance, receiving ordinances, and allowing the Spirit to change us through Christ's grace.

## Questions to sit with

- What would you hope the phrase "walked with God" meant in the story of your own life?
- What is worth remembering or recording for someone who comes after you?
- How does Enoch's message move us beyond the question of his personal qualifications?
- What does spiritual rebirth add to the idea of simply trying harder?
