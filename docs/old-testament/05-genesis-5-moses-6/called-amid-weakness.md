# Called amid Weakness - I Will Be with Thee

## Enoch's first response

> 31. "Why is it that I have found favor in thy sight, and am but a lad, and all the people hate me; for I am slow of speech; wherefore am I thy servant?"

Moses 6:31, excerpt.

[Read Moses 6:26-32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/6?lang=eng&id=p26-p32#p26)

Enoch does not begin by listing his qualifications. He describes youth, rejection, and difficulty speaking. The Lord's answer is not an argument that Enoch has imagined every limitation. It is a promise of help and a call to act.

The word "lad" expresses Enoch's concern, but we should not turn it into a modern claim about his exact age. Verse 25 places his call within the chronology of the account, which differs from ordinary expectations about age today.

## Other servants recognize their limits

| Servant | Concern | The Lord's response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enoch | Young, disliked, and slow of speech | He will receive utterance; the Lord invites him to walk with Him. |
| Moses | Not eloquent; slow of speech | The Lord will be with his mouth and teach him what to say. |
| Gideon | His family is poor and he is the least in his father's house | The Lord promises to be with him. |
| Jeremiah | He cannot speak and regards himself as a child | The Lord sends him, gives words, and promises deliverance. |
| Isaiah | He recognizes his unclean lips | He receives cleansing before answering the call to go. |

[Read Exodus 4:10-16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/4?lang=eng&id=p10-p16#p10)

[Read Judges 6:15-16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/judg/6?lang=eng&id=p15-p16#p15)

[Read Jeremiah 1:6-9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/1?lang=eng&id=p6-p9#p6)

[Read Isaiah 6:5-8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/6?lang=eng&id=p5-p8#p5)

The concerns are not all the same. Isaiah's need for cleansing is different from Moses' difficulty speaking or Gideon's concern about status. Do not treat disability, low social standing, and sin as interchangeable forms of deficiency.

## The recurring promise is God's presence

For Moses, the account also includes Aaron's help. Receiving assistance does not invalidate a calling. Preparation, collaboration, accommodations, and learning can all belong to a faithful response.

We do not need to claim the same prophetic commission or miraculous protection to learn from these accounts. Their shared invitation is to stop treating our first assessment of ourselves as the final measure of what is possible with God.

## Questions to sit with

- Which concern is easiest for you to understand, and why?
- What does the Lord offer besides a command to be more confident?
- How does Isaiah's cleansing differ from the help offered with other limitations?
- What support could make an act of service possible without requiring us to pretend we have no needs?
