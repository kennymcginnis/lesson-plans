# Moses 6:35-36 - Seeing a Wider View

## Enoch is invited to see

The Lord tells Enoch to anoint his eyes with clay and wash them. Enoch obeys, and the account describes what follows:

> 36. "He beheld also things which were not visible to the natural eye; and from thenceforth came the saying abroad in the land: A seer hath the Lord raised up unto his people."

Moses 6:36, excerpt.

[Read Moses 6:35-36](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/6?lang=eng&id=p35-p36#p35)

Enoch's seeing is presented as a gift from the Lord, not simply unusually sharp observation. The account invites us to recognize that our own immediate view is incomplete.

## What is a seer?

Mosiah 8:13-18 provides a companion explanation of a seer and the revealing of things otherwise unknown. Read it alongside Enoch's experience rather than reducing a seer to someone who makes successful predictions.

[Read Mosiah 8:13-18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/8?lang=eng&id=p13-p18#p13)

Prophetic perspective can help us see our choices in relation to covenants, consequences, and eternal purposes. Receiving counsel involves more than admiring someone else's vision. We study, pray, seek understanding, and consider what faithful action would look like in our circumstances.

## A wider view is not a claim to know everything

The Lord gives Enoch particular things to see and a particular message to deliver. These verses do not say that every opinion expressed by a religious leader is revelation or that following counsel prevents every mortal loss.

Nor should we replace our responsibility to discern and act with the assumption that another person must make every decision for us. Humility about our limited view can coexist with sincere questions and careful judgment.

## Questions to sit with

- What does Moses 6 say the Lord enabled Enoch to see?
- When has counsel helped you recognize a consequence or possibility you had overlooked?
- How can we seek confirmation and understanding rather than merely repeat a familiar phrase?
- What counsel deserves more thoughtful attention in your life right now?
