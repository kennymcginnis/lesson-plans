# Ecclesiastes 3 - A Time and a Season

## Ecclesiastes 3:1, 4, 6

> 1. To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven:
>
> 4. A time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to mourn, and a time to dance;
>
> 6. A time to get, and a time to lose; a time to keep, and a time to cast away;

[Read Ecclesiastes 3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/eccl/3?lang=eng)

## Life does not stay in one season

Ecclesiastes 1-2 questions what we can ultimately gain from achievement, possessions, and work. Chapter 3 makes room for experiences we cannot all have at once: mourning and dancing, keeping and letting go.

We do not need to rush someone out of grief to affirm faith. The passage gives mourning its own place.

### Claudio R. M. Costa - That They Do Always Remember Him

**Direct quotation: Ecclesiastes 3:1, 6.** Elder Costa, of the Seventy, explains:

> "I believe the Lord has His own timing as to when to give revelation unto us."

He says he understood this while studying these verses, then quotes them. In a message about remembering the Savior, he connects the passage to the timing of revelation.

["That They Do Always Remember Him," October 2015](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2015/10/that-they-do-always-remember-him?lang=eng)

## Waiting does not always mean standing still

[Ecclesiastes 11:4-6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/eccl/11?lang=eng&id=p4-p6#p4) offers a useful companion reading from the assignment: uncertainty about wind, weather, and results need not keep us from sowing.

This is a discussion connection between the assigned passages, not an additional scripture citation attributed to Elder Costa.

## Questions to sit with

- What does this passage give us permission to feel?
- When has understanding come later than you hoped?
- How can we respect another person's season without insisting it look like ours?
- What can we faithfully do while we wait for something we cannot control?
