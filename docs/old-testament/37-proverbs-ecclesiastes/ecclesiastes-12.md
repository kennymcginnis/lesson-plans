# Ecclesiastes 12 - Reverence, Trust, and Duty

## Ecclesiastes 12:13-14

> 13. Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.
> 14. For God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether it be good, or whether it be evil.

[Read Ecclesiastes 12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/eccl/12?lang=eng&id=p13-p14#p13)

## What does it mean to fear God?

After Ecclesiastes explores life's limits and uncertainties, its conclusion points toward God. But "fear God" can sound very different from "trust God." How do those invitations belong together?

### David A. Bednar - Therefore They Hushed Their Fears

**Direct quotation: Ecclesiastes 12:13-14.** Elder Bednar, of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, quotes both verses and explains:

> "Godly fear is loving and trusting in Him. As we fear God more completely, we love Him more perfectly."

He describes reverence, respect, and awe for Jesus Christ. His teaching also points to our dependence on the Savior's mercy and grace, not merely our own capacity or performance.

["Therefore They Hushed Their Fears," April 2015](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2015/04/therefore-they-hushed-their-fears?lang=eng)

### Thomas S. Monson - True to Our Priesthood Trust

**Direct quotation: Ecclesiastes 12:13.** President Monson, then First Counselor in the First Presidency, calls this "a favorite scripture" and says:

> "I love, I cherish the noble word duty."

In a priesthood address, he uses the verse to introduce the importance of fulfilling our responsibilities to God and others.

["True to Our Priesthood Trust," October 2006](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2006/10/true-to-our-priesthood-trust?lang=eng)
