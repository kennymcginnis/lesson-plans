# Closing - Let Him Direct Your Path

## Return to the opening question

> What does it mean to let God direct your path when you cannot yet see where it leads?

Proverbs and Ecclesiastes do not remove every uncertainty. They give us ways to live faithfully within it.

- Trust the Lord without pretending to have every answer. (Proverbs 3:5-7)
- Seek wisdom and notice growing light. (Proverbs 4:7, 18)
- Let truth and gentleness shape your words. (Proverbs 15:1; 16:24)
- Teach with love while honoring agency. (Proverbs 22:6)
- Recognize strength, kindness, and reverence. (Proverbs 31:20, 26, 30)
- Make room for different seasons of life. (Ecclesiastes 3:1-8)
- Let love of God give meaning to duty. (Ecclesiastes 12:13-14)

## One step this week

Invite each person to choose one passage and one concrete way to act on it. They can keep that choice private.

It might be a prayer about an unanswered question, a gentler response, time spent listening to a child, or an expression of gratitude for someone whose goodness often goes unnoticed.

> What is one next step you can take with the light you already have?
