# Proverbs 22:6 - Teach Without Controlling

## Proverbs 22:6

> 6. Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

[Read Proverbs 22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/prov/22?lang=eng&id=p6#p6)

## Teaching is a relationship, not control of an outcome

This proverb gives weight to what children learn early. Read it alongside the reality of individual agency, not as permission to judge parents by an adult child's decisions.

### Russell M. Nelson - Salvation and Exaltation

**Direct quotation: Proverbs 22:6, in footnote 19.** Elder Nelson, then of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, introduces the verse in the note with "Remember that parental proverb" and quotes it in full.

In the talk itself, he teaches:

> "Do not try to control your children. Instead, listen to them, help them to learn the gospel, inspire them, and lead them toward eternal life."

His counsel on correction emphasizes doing it quietly, privately, and lovingly. The point is persuasion rooted in sincere love, not merely securing outward compliance.

["Salvation and Exaltation," April 2008](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2008/04/salvation-and-exaltation?lang=eng)

## Questions to sit with

- What did you learn from a parent's or teacher's example that stayed with you?
- How does listening change the way we teach?
- What is the difference between helping someone choose and choosing for them?
- How can we support parents whose children have taken a different path?

These questions can include grandparents, teachers, mentors, and others who help children grow. No one needs to share private family concerns to participate.
