# Proverbs 31 - Strength, Wisdom, and Reverence

## Proverbs 31:10, 20, 26, 30-31

> 10. Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies.
>
>
> 20. She stretcheth out her hand to the poor; yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy.
>
>
> 26. She openeth her mouth with wisdom; and in her tongue is the law of kindness.
>
>
> 30. Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain: but a woman that feareth the Lord, she shall be praised.
> 31. Give her of the fruit of her hands; and let her own works praise her in the gates.

[Read Proverbs 31:10-31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/prov/31?lang=eng&id=p10-p31#p10)

## Notice what the passage praises

The portrait includes initiative, strength, work, generosity, wisdom, kindness, and reverence for God. The lesson manual describes a woman of great spiritual strength, capability, and influence and asks which traits we can emulate.

Consider the passage as an invitation to recognize and honor character, not as a checklist for comparing women or measuring their worth by productivity.

### David A. Bednar - Therefore They Hushed Their Fears

**Curriculum connection: Proverbs 31:30.** The manual includes this verse in its group of passages about fearing the Lord and recommends Elder Bednar's teaching:

> "Godly fear is loving and trusting in Him."

Elder Bednar, of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, directly quotes Ecclesiastes 12:13-14 in this talk. The connection to Proverbs 31:30 is made by the curriculum; it is not presented here as a direct quotation of Proverbs.

## Questions to sit with

- Which qualities in this passage do you recognize in a woman who has influenced you?
- How do wisdom and kindness belong together in verse 26?
- What does verse 30 suggest about the things we praise or overlook?
- How could we give more recognition to quiet generosity and spiritual strength?
