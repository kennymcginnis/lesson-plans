# He Shall Direct Thy Paths

September 7-13, 2026 - Proverbs and Ecclesiastes

This week's assigned reading is Proverbs 1-4; 15-16; 22; 31; Ecclesiastes 1-3; 11-12.

> What does it mean to let God direct your path when you cannot yet see where it leads?

Proverbs offers practical wisdom for walking that path. Ecclesiastes asks what lasts when our accomplishments, possessions, and time do not. Together they invite us to trust God and let that trust change the way we live.
