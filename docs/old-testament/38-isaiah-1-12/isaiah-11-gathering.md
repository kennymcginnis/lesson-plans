# Isaiah 11:10-12 - An Ensign for the Nations

## Isaiah 11:12

> 12. And he shall set up an ensign for the nations, and shall assemble the outcasts of Israel, and gather together the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.

[Read Isaiah 11:10-12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/11?lang=eng&id=p10-p12#p10)

## A gathering point for scattered people

An ensign is a flag or standard around which people gather. Isaiah describes the Lord recovering His people, including those who are outcast and dispersed. Notice the reach of the invitation: nations and the four corners of the earth.

The Lord is the initiator of this work. Our role in gathering is to help people come to Him, not to make ourselves the center of their faith.

### Moroni and Joseph Smith

**Restoration connection: Joseph Smith-History 1:40, 45-46, 49.** Joseph Smith records that Moroni quoted Isaiah 11 and said that it was about to be fulfilled. His account also describes Moroni repeating his message during later visits.

This is more than a modern thematic resemblance. Isaiah 11 was explicitly part of the message that introduced Joseph Smith to his work.

[Read Joseph Smith-History 1:40](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/js-h/1?lang=eng&id=p40#p40)

### The 2026 Seminary lesson

**Curriculum interpretation: Isaiah 11:10-12.** The teacher manual identifies the recovery in verse 11 with the latter-day gathering of Israel. It connects the ensign in verse 12 with the restored Church, including its ordinances and covenants, and teaches:

> "The Lord's restored Church is an ensign to gather people to the gospel of Jesus Christ."

Read D&C 113:5-6 alongside the passage for the connection between priesthood keys and gathering. D&C 45:9 also connects the everlasting covenant with being a light and standard to the nations.

### Boyd K. Packer - A Defense and a Refuge

**Explicit citation and interpretation: Isaiah 11:12.** President Packer, then Acting President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, cites Isaiah while discussing homes, refuge, and the gathering. He makes the focus unmistakable:

> "The ensign to which all of us are to rally is Jesus Christ"

His address moves from the pioneers at Ensign Peak to ordinary people living the gospel in their families. Gathering is not only a distant institutional project; it has a place in the way we live and welcome others.

["A Defense and a Refuge," October 2006](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2006/10/a-defense-and-a-refuge?lang=eng)

## Questions to sit with

- What do the words "outcasts" and "dispersed" teach us about whom the Lord remembers?
- What difference does it make that Moroni specifically quoted this chapter?
- How can we keep invitations centered on Jesus Christ rather than on our own expectations?
- What could gathering look like through friendship, sharing faith, or temple and family history service this week?

Respect another person's agency when extending an invitation. Love and friendship should not depend on whether it is accepted.
