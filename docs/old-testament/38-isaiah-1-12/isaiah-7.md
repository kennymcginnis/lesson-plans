# Isaiah 7:14 - Immanuel, God with Us

## Isaiah 7:14

> 14. Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.

[Read Isaiah 7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/7?lang=eng&id=p14#p14)

## A promise spoken into fear

Isaiah 7 begins with a frightened king and people facing a military threat. Read verses 1-9 before moving to verse 14. Isaiah's call to faith is addressed to people who have real reasons to be afraid.

For this lesson, follow Matthew's explicit application of the prophecy to Jesus Christ. Matthew 1:21-23 connects the promised birth with Jesus saving His people from their sins and explains Emmanuel as "God with us."

[Read Matthew 1:21-23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/1?lang=eng&id=p21-p23#p21)

Isaiah's original historical setting still matters. The 2026 Come, Follow Me lesson explains that prophecies can have more than one fulfillment. We do not need to settle every question about the immediate sign to Ahaz in order to study the New Testament's witness of Christ.

## Jesus Christ came to be our Savior

**Scriptural interpretation: Matthew 1:21-23.** Matthew directly quotes the prophecy and applies it to the birth of Jesus. This connection rests on scripture itself, not on an inferred conference citation.

Read Luke 1:31-33 alongside Isaiah 9:6-7 as a companion passage. Gabriel speaks of Jesus receiving David's throne and reigning without end. Together these passages turn our attention from a title alone to the identity and saving work of the promised Son.

[Read Luke 1:31-33](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/1?lang=eng&id=p31-p33#p31)

## Questions to sit with

- What fears surround the promise in Isaiah 7? What does that add to our reading?
- How does "God with us" differ from a promise that nothing difficult will happen?
- What does Matthew tell us Jesus came to save us from?
- How could remembering the Savior's presence change the way we face one concern this week?
