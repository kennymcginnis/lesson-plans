# Come Now

Isaiah 1-12

[Open the presentation](presentations/isaiah-1-12/index.html ':ignore')

You do not have to wait until you are better to turn to God. Explore Isaiah's invitation through the slides or read [Scarlet to Snow](old-testament/38-isaiah-1-12/isaiah-1.md).

As you reflect, consider one step toward God you can take now. Personal responses can stay private.

## What do you expect God to do?

<details>
<summary>Show an example answer</summary>

A reader might expect Isaiah to be rejected because he acknowledges his uncleanness. Instead, Isaiah 6:6-8 describes cleansing followed by an invitation to serve. This is one possible expectation to compare with the story, not the only right response.

</details>
