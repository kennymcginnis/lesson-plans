# Closing - Rehearse for Rejoicing

## The question and the hope

Habakkuk asks how violence and injustice can continue. Nahum announces that oppressive power will not have the final word. Zephaniah describes a people restored to truthful speech, safety, belonging, and joy in God's presence.

The books do not remove every question about how or when the Lord will act. They invite us to live faithfully toward a future in which His justice and saving love prevail.

> "Yet I will rejoice in the Lord, I will joy in the God of my salvation."

Habakkuk 3:18, KJV.

[Read Habakkuk 3:17-19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/hab/3?lang=eng&id=p17-p19#p17)

## Remember the three voices

- **Nahum:** The Lord is good, knows those who trust Him, and offers refuge in trouble.
- **Habakkuk:** Honest lament can lead into faithful waiting without pretending that every loss has ended.
- **Zephaniah:** The Lord gathers His people, restores dignity, and rejoices over them.

In Jesus Christ, we look for the fullness of deliverance and restored life. That hope is larger than our present circumstances, but it should make us more attentive to those circumstances, not less compassionate toward suffering.

## Rehearse the kind of life we hope for

If we hope for truthful speech, practice it. If we hope for the gathering of the excluded, welcome someone. If we hope for peace and justice, refuse cruelty and help repair harm within our reach.

We cannot create the entire promised future through our own effort. These choices are responses to God's grace and signs of what we trust Him to accomplish.

Read Zephaniah 3:14 and 17 together. The people sing, and the Lord rejoices over them. Let that shared joy be an image of belonging, not pressure to appear cheerful when we are hurting.

[Read Zephaniah 3:14-17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/zeph/3?lang=eng&id=p14-p17#p14)

## Questions to sit with

- Which promise would you like to carry into the week?
- What question can you bring honestly to God rather than keep outside your prayers?
- What is one way to live toward the peace, truthfulness, or welcome these prophets describe?

Allow a private response without asking class members to disclose trauma, family conflict, or financial distress. Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ and the hope we have in His saving work.
