# Return to Living Water

Jeremiah 1–3; 7; 16–18; 20 · October 19–25, 2026

[Open the Slidev presentation](presentations/jeremiah-1-20/index.html ':ignore')

The 25-minute class follows [Jeremiah's call](old-testament/43-jeremiah-part-1/jeremiah-1.md), the warning about hollow worship in chapter 7, the [fountain of living waters](old-testament/43-jeremiah-part-1/jeremiah-2.md), the [invitation to return](old-testament/43-jeremiah-part-1/jeremiah-3.md), [the potter's work](old-testament/43-jeremiah-part-1/jeremiah-18.md), and [the fire in Jeremiah's bones](old-testament/43-jeremiah-part-1/jeremiah-20.md). Slides 1–10 form the teaching path. Slide 11 offers optional follow-up on chapters 16 and 17.

The shared [YouTube lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MzxgjQLWUtY) informs this class path. Its transcript frames Jeremiah's story with the fire in his bones (0:00–4:04), then moves through **the call** (6:43), **the counterfeit** (10:37), **the choice** (19:38), and **the clay** (30:10). The slides use those themes and cite the scriptural passages and [official study guide](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-old-testament-2026/43?lang=eng) directly.

Invite students to discuss the passages and choose a private next step. They do not need to disclose a personal fear, sin, or painful experience.
