# Closing - Return and Be Renewed

## Put down roots by the water

> "Blessed is the man that trusteth in the Lord, and whose hope the Lord is."

Jeremiah 17:7, KJV.

[Read Jeremiah 17:7-8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/17?lang=eng&id=p7-p8#p7)

The tree in verse 8 still lives through heat and drought. Its hope lies in where its roots reach. Jeremiah's invitation is not to pretend life is easy but to return to the One who can sustain us.

## Remember the images

- **A prophet's mouth:** The Lord knows Jeremiah's limits and promises help for the work.
- **Living water:** God is the source, not one more substitute that cannot sustain us.
- **Returning children:** Acknowledgment and mercy belong together in repentance.
- **A passing harvest:** A needed response should not always be postponed to another season.
- **The potter's clay:** A damaged past need not be the end of the story; our response matters.
- **A fire within:** Conviction can remain real even when service is difficult and prayer includes lament.

## Questions to sit with

- Which image helps you recognize something you need from the Savior?
- Is there a substitute for God you need to stop asking to sustain you?
- What one act of returning, repairing, or receiving help could begin today?

Give time for a private response. No one needs to explain a personal sin, family conflict, or painful experience publicly.

## One response for the week

Choose a small action: pray honestly, make a needed apology, prepare for worship with a specific question, ask for support, or offer help to someone who is struggling. Let the action express a return to Christ rather than an attempt to prove that you no longer need Him.

Close with your own testimony of His mercy and sustaining grace. We can bring both our willingness and our unfinished growth to Him.
