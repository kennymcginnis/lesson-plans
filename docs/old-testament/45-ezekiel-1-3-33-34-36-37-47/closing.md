# Closing - One Act of Care

The Lord met Ezekiel in exile, called him to speak, promised to seek His sheep, and showed life returning to a people who said their hope was lost. His promises lead toward a new heart and healing that reaches beyond one place.

[Read Ezekiel 34:15–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/34?lang=eng&id=p15-p16#p15) or [36:26–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/36?lang=eng&id=p26-p27#p26).

Invite a private response: choose one person to care for, one passage to revisit, or one need for renewal to bring to the Lord. Sharing is optional.

**What is one small response you could begin this week?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>I could ask a neighbor what support would help, then listen to the answer. Or I could pray about an area where I need the Lord to soften my heart.</p></details>

Close with a brief witness of Jesus Christ, the good shepherd, and the Lord's power to restore His people.
