# A New Heart and Living Bones (Ezekiel 36–37)

The Lord promises to gather and cleanse His people, give them a heart of flesh, and put His Spirit within them. The promise does not ask people to manufacture a new heart by willpower alone. It invites trust in the Lord's renewing work and a willing response.

[Read Ezekiel 36:24–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/36?lang=eng&id=p24-p27#p24).

In Ezekiel 37, dry bones come together and receive breath. The Lord explains the symbol: “these bones are the whole house of Israel,” who say their hope is lost. The Lord promises to bring them into their land and put His Spirit in them. The image can encourage someone who feels hopeless, but avoid claiming that every painful circumstance will improve at once.

[Read Ezekiel 37:1–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/37?lang=eng&id=p1-p14#p1).
