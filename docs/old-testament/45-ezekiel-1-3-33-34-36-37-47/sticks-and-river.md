# One People and Healing Water (Ezekiel 37; 47)

The Lord tells Ezekiel to join sticks marked Judah and Joseph. He explains that He will gather the divided children of Israel and make them one people. The [Come, Follow Me guide](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-old-testament-2026/45?lang=eng) also invites us to see the Bible and Book of Mormon as companion witnesses that help gather people to Christ. The text's explanation of a reunited people and that latter-day application can be discussed together without treating them as identical claims.

[Read Ezekiel 37:15–23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/37?lang=eng&id=p15-p23#p15).

In Ezekiel 47, water flows from the temple toward a barren place. Where it goes, living things flourish; the trees bear fruit and their leaves are for healing. The guide invites reflection on spiritual healing through the Lord's house. Christian readers may also connect living water to Christ (see John 4:14), while identifying that as an application beyond Ezekiel's explicit wording.

[Read Ezekiel 47:1–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/47?lang=eng&id=p1-p12#p1) and [John 4:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/4?lang=eng&id=p14#p14).

## Questions to sit with

**What does the Lord say the joined sticks mean?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>He will gather Israel from the nations and make the divided people one in His hand (Ezekiel 37:19–22).</p></details>

**How can two books of scripture help gather people to Christ?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>The Bible and Book of Mormon can witness of Jesus Christ together and invite people to follow Him; this is an application highlighted by the study guide.</p></details>

**What changes as the water flows from the temple?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>The waters are healed, living things thrive, and trees provide fruit and leaves for healing (Ezekiel 47:8–12).</p></details>
