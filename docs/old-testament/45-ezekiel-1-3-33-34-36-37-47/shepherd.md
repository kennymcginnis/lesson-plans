# The Shepherd Who Seeks (Ezekiel 34)

Ezekiel 34 rebukes leaders who feed themselves while neglecting the flock. The weak are not strengthened, the sick not healed, and the lost not sought. Then the Lord says He Himself will seek, gather, feed, bind up, and strengthen His sheep.

[Read Ezekiel 34:2–6, 11–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/34?lang=eng&id=p2-p6,p11-p16#p2).

The promised “one shepherd” in verse 23 points Christian readers toward Jesus Christ, who calls Himself the good shepherd in John 10:11. Discuss acts of care without assuming that someone who misses church wants repeated visits. Ask what help they welcome and respect their answer.

[Read Ezekiel 34:23–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/34?lang=eng&id=p23-p24#p23) and [John 10:11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/10?lang=eng&id=p11#p11).

## Questions to sit with

**What had the shepherds failed to do?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>They fed themselves but had not strengthened the weak, healed the sick, bound up the broken, brought back the driven away, or sought the lost (Ezekiel 34:2–6).</p></details>

**Which verbs describe what the Lord promises to do?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>He says He will seek, deliver, gather, feed, bind up, and strengthen His sheep (Ezekiel 34:11–16).</p></details>

**What is one act of shepherding that respects another person’s choices?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>We could ask how they are doing, offer a specific kind of help, listen to what they want, and honor a refusal.</p></details>
