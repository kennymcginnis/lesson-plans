# The Lord Is There (Ezekiel 1–3)

Ezekiel is among the exiles by the river Chebar when he sees the Lord's glory. The vision's creatures and wheels are striking, but its first teaching point is simpler: the Lord can reach His people even there. Ezekiel falls on his face, then is called to speak to a rebellious people.

[Read Ezekiel 1:1, 26–28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/1?lang=eng&id=p1-p1,p26-p28#p1) and [2:1–3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/2?lang=eng&id=p1-p3#p1).

The Lord tells Ezekiel to eat a scroll before speaking His words. He must receive the message himself, not merely repeat it. Ezekiel 3:15 says he sat among the exiles for seven days. That detail can open a discussion about listening before speaking.

[Read Ezekiel 3:1–3, 15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/3?lang=eng&id=p1-p3,p15#p1).
