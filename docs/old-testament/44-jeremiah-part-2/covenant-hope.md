# A Covenant Written on the Heart (Jeremiah 31–33)

## Mourning is not the final word

Jeremiah 31 speaks to a people who will experience exile and grief. The Lord promises gathering, comfort, and joy. He does not ask them to pretend the loss is small. Read the promise of joy together with the chapter's image of bitter weeping.

[Read Jeremiah 31:10–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/31?lang=eng&id=p10-p17#p10)

The week's [Come, Follow Me guide](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-old-testament-2026/44?lang=eng) draws attention to the Lord's work of building and planting after ruin. Ask: **What does the Lord promise to do, and what does He ask His people to receive or become?**

## The new covenant

In Jeremiah 31:31–34, the Lord promises a new covenant with Israel and Judah. He describes His law within the people and His forgiveness of their iniquity. Jeremiah 32:37–42 adds promises of gathering, a united heart, and an everlasting covenant.

[Read Jeremiah 31:31–34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/31?lang=eng&id=p31-p34#p31)

[Read Jeremiah 32:37–42](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/32?lang=eng&id=p37-p42#p37)

The contrast with the burned scroll is a teaching connection, not an equation Jeremiah himself states: words can be destroyed on one scroll, yet the Lord promises to write His law on hearts. Inner change includes our willing response and His forgiving grace; it is more than displaying religious identity.

## The Branch of righteousness

Jeremiah 33:14–16 promises a righteous Branch from David's line who will bring judgment and righteousness. Christian readers see this promise in relation to Jesus Christ. The transcript closes with the new covenant and Christ at about 37:35–38:21; the passage gives a grounded way to make that connection.

[Read Jeremiah 33:14–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/33?lang=eng&id=p14-p16#p14)

## Questions to sit with

- What is different about a law written on the heart rather than displayed only outwardly?
- Which promises in Jeremiah 31–33 speak to people who feel that loss has ended their story?
- What does the promised Branch reveal about the Lord's way of restoring His people?
- How can a covenant relationship shape the next small decision we make?
