# Closing - A Faithful Next Step

Jeremiah's generation did not receive the outcome they wanted. The burned scroll was written again; Ebed-melech helped Jeremiah out of the cistern; Zedekiah feared the cost of acting; and the city fell. Against that grief, the Lord promised a covenant written on hearts and a righteous Branch.

[Read Jeremiah 31:31–34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/31?lang=eng&id=p31-p34#p31) and [Lamentations 3:21–26](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/lam/3?lang=eng&id=p21-p26#p21).

## A quiet invitation

Invite students to choose one response they can begin this week:

- Listen to a passage they have avoided or misunderstood, and ask what it actually says.
- Seek counsel with willingness to hear an unwelcome answer.
- Offer practical help to someone in difficulty, as Ebed-melech did.
- Bring grief honestly to the Lord while holding to His mercy.

No one needs to share a private decision or explain a painful experience publicly. Close with a brief witness of Jesus Christ, the promised Branch, whose covenant offers forgiveness and renewal.
