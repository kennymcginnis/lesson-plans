# Hear, Act, and Hope

Jeremiah 31–33; 36–39; Lamentations 1; 3 · October 26–November 1, 2026

[Open the Slidev presentation](presentations/jeremiah-31-39/index.html ':ignore')

The 25-minute path is slides 1–11. It follows the [burned scroll](old-testament/44-jeremiah-part-2/burning-scroll.md), [Zedekiah's hesitation](old-testament/44-jeremiah-part-2/zedekiah.md), [Ebed-melech's rescue](old-testament/44-jeremiah-part-2/ebed-melech.md), the [new covenant](old-testament/44-jeremiah-part-2/covenant-hope.md), and [hope within lament](old-testament/44-jeremiah-part-2/lamentations.md). Slide 12 is optional background from Jeremiah 27–28 and 40–43.

The presentation follows the assigned chapters and the [official study guide](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-old-testament-2026/44?lang=eng). Each discussion slide reveals one possible answer on the next click; invite class responses before revealing it.

Discussion prompts do not require a student to disclose a private decision or painful experience. The presenter notes contain timing, context, and scripture links.
