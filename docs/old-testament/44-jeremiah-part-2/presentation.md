The supplied transcript begins its teaching at about 0:56 and asks what we do when God's word is hard to accept. It covers the scroll (1:28), Hananiah (5:35), Zedekiah and Ebed-melech (10:45–23:41), the later Egypt story (27:28), and hope in Christ (36:26). The presentation draws on those themes while giving the assigned chapters and the [official study guide](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-old-testament-2026/44?lang=eng) priority.

Discussion prompts do not require a student to disclose a private decision or painful experience. The presenter notes contain timing, context, and scripture links.
