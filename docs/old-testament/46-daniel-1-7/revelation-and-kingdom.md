# Pray, Receive, and Thank (Daniel 2)

When Nebuchadnezzar demands the dream and its meaning, Daniel asks for time and gathers his friends to seek mercy from God. When the mystery is revealed, Daniel praises God before explaining it to the king. He gives God credit rather than treating revelation as proof of his own brilliance.

[Read Daniel 2:16–23, 27–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/2?lang=eng&id=p16-p23,p27-p30#p16).

The dream's stone becomes a great mountain and fills the earth. Daniel explains that God will establish a kingdom that will not be destroyed. The [Come, Follow Me guide](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-old-testament-2026/46?lang=eng) connects this latter-day kingdom with the Church of Jesus Christ. Keep the focus on God's enduring work rather than a detailed chart assigning each part of the statue to modern nations.

[Read Daniel 2:31–35, 44–45](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/2?lang=eng&id=p31-p35,p44-p45#p31).

## Questions to sit with

**What does Daniel do before he has an answer?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>He asks for time and invites his friends to seek God’s mercy with him (Daniel 2:16–18).</p></details>

**What does Daniel do after the answer comes?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>He blesses and thanks God, then tells the king that God reveals mysteries (Daniel 2:19–23, 27–28).</p></details>

**What does the stone teach about the kingdom God establishes?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>It is not made by human hands, becomes a great mountain, and represents a kingdom God sets up that will not be destroyed (Daniel 2:34–35, 44–45).</p></details>
