# Resolve and Respect (Daniel 1)

Daniel and his friends are taken into Babylonian service and offered the king's food. Daniel resolves not to defile himself. He does not insult the official or demand a privilege; he requests a test and accepts that the official has a concern about their health. God gives the four knowledge and skill. The passage does not specify every reason the royal food troubled Daniel, so avoid treating a guessed explanation as the text's claim.

[Read Daniel 1:8–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/1?lang=eng&id=p8-p17#p8).
