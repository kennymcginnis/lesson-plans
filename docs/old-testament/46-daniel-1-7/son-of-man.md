# The Son of Man and an Enduring Kingdom (Daniel 7)

Daniel sees troubling beasts representing earthly kingdoms, then a heavenly court and one “like the Son of man” receiving dominion, glory, and a kingdom that will not pass away. The [study guide](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-old-testament-2026/46?lang=eng) invites readers to consider Jesus Christ in Daniel 7:13–14 and His use of “Son of man” in Mark 14:61–64. The vision's reassurance rests in His enduring rule, not in confidently identifying every beast with a present-day power.

[Read Daniel 7:9–14, 17–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/7?lang=eng&id=p9-p14,p17-p18#p9) and [Mark 14:61–64](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/14?lang=eng&id=p61-p64#p61).

**How does Jesus’ use of this title help us read Daniel 7?**
