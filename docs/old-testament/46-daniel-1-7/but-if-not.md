# But If Not (Daniel 3)

Nebuchadnezzar commands worship of a golden image. Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego refuse. They trust that God can deliver them, yet say that even if He does not, they will not worship the image. Read their answer before reading their rescue: the power of their statement does not depend on knowing the ending.

[Read Daniel 3:13–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/3?lang=eng&id=p13-p18#p13).

The king later sees a fourth figure in the fire, and the three men emerge unharmed. The text gives Nebuchadnezzar's description of the figure; it does not require the class to settle the figure's identity. Faith in Christ can sustain people through a trial, but this narrative is not a promise that every faithful person will survive every danger.

[Read Daniel 3:24–28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/3?lang=eng&id=p24-p28#p24).

## Questions to sit with

**What are the three friends certain God can do?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>They say God is able to deliver them from the furnace and the king’s hand (Daniel 3:17).</p></details>

**What do the words “but if not” add to their testimony?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>They will worship God even if the rescue they hope for does not come (Daniel 3:18).</p></details>

**How can we encourage faith without promising a particular outcome?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>We can testify of God’s presence and help, acknowledge uncertainty, and support someone through the actual result.</p></details>
