# Prayer as a Steady Practice (Daniel 6)

Other officials persuade Darius to prohibit petitions to anyone but the king for thirty days. Daniel knows the decree but continues his regular practice of praying and giving thanks to God. The chapter emphasizes what he had already done “aforetime.” His prayer is a steady habit, not a sudden performance for an audience.

[Read Daniel 6:7–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/6?lang=eng&id=p7-p10#p7).

Daniel is cast into the lions' den and later says God sent His angel to shut the lions' mouths. The king's relief is real, but the passage should not be used to promise identical deliverance to every believer. Daniel's choice can also prompt discussion about how to worship faithfully and wisely in different circumstances.

[Read Daniel 6:16–23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/6?lang=eng&id=p16-p23#p16).
