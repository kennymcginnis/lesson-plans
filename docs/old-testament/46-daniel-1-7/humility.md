# Two Kings and Humility (Daniel 4–5)

Daniel urges Nebuchadnezzar to turn from his sins and show mercy to the poor. The king boasts about his own power, then loses it. Later, he acknowledges the rule of heaven. Daniel 5 presents Belshazzar as a contrast: Daniel says Belshazzar knew what happened to Nebuchadnezzar yet did not humble his heart. At a feast, he uses vessels taken from the temple while praising other gods; a written judgment appears on the wall.

[Read Daniel 4:27, 30, 34–37](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/4?lang=eng&id=p27,p30,p34-p37#p27) and [5:20–23, 25–28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/5?lang=eng&id=p20-p23,p25-p28#p20).

These accounts concern two rulers with power over others. Invite application to our own pride without equating mental illness, disability, or ordinary setbacks with punishment from God.
