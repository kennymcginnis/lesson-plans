# Closing - One Faithful Choice

Daniel 1 shows a resolved heart; Daniel 2 shows friends praying and giving thanks; Daniel 3 leaves room for “but if not”; Daniel 6 shows prayer maintained over time; and Daniel 7 points to a kingdom that will not pass away.

[Read Daniel 3:17–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/3?lang=eng&id=p17-p18#p17) and [7:13–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/7?lang=eng&id=p13-p14#p13).

Invite students to choose one quiet response for the week. They might resolve how to meet an ordinary pressure, ask a friend to pray with them, give God credit for help, or return to a regular prayer practice. Sharing is optional.

**What is one faithful choice you can begin before the next hard moment?**

<details><summary>Show a sample answer</summary><p>I could choose a daily time to pray, so that seeking God is already part of my life when pressure comes.</p></details>

Close with a brief witness of Jesus Christ, whose kingdom endures and who can sustain His people even when the outcome is uncertain.
