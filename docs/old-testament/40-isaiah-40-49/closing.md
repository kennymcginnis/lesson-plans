# Closing - Remember the Savior

## The God who is great also remembers

Isaiah asks who can measure the waters in God's hand, then speaks of His people engraved upon His palms. Greatness and personal care belong together. The Lord is not too powerful to notice need or too distant to sustain the weary.

> "Yet will I not forget thee."

Isaiah 49:15, KJV excerpt.

[Read Isaiah 49:14-16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/49?lang=eng&id=p14-p16#p14)

## Remember the movement

- **Comfort:** The Lord speaks hope to a people who need restoration.
- **A way forward:** His provision includes sustenance along the journey, not only a destination.
- **Renewed strength:** We can wait with trust, receive help, and take the next faithful step.
- **The Creator:** Useful resources cannot replace the One who redeems us.
- **Light for others:** God's saving purpose reaches beyond our own circle.
- **Remembrance:** Feelings of abandonment can be brought to the Lord who promises not to forget.

These promises do not require us to turn every tragedy into a quick success story. Faith can include waiting, lament, and help from others while we trust the Savior's final redeeming work.

## Questions to sit with

> What has Jesus Christ sacrificed for you?

- Which image in these chapters helps you receive His comfort?
- Where do you need to wait with hope rather than assume that you have been forgotten?
- How could remembering His gift change one response to another person?

Allow a quiet moment for reflection. Class members need not share a private loss or painful family experience to participate.

## One response for the week

Choose one action: return to a sustaining scripture, pray about a fear, accept needed support, reconsider a misplaced source of security, or contact someone who may feel overlooked.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. The invitation is to trust and walk with Him, not to prove that we can manage the journey without Him.
