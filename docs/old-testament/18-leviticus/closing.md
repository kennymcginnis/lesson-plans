# Closing - Come to the Throne of Grace

## An invitation to approach

> 16. "Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need."

Hebrews 4:16, KJV.

[Read Hebrews 4:15-16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/4?lang=eng&id=p15-p16#p15)

The invitation follows a description of a high priest who understands our weaknesses. Confidence here is not confidence that we have never failed. It is confidence in Jesus Christ and His willingness to give mercy and help.

## Remember the four themes

- **Set apart:** Belong to the Lord, and let that belonging shape worship, honesty, generosity, and love of neighbor.
- **Sacrifice:** Offer yourself wholeheartedly, willingly, and daily, remembering that your efforts respond to Christ's gift rather than purchase it.
- **Purity:** Look to the Savior for cleansing, and treat people with the compassion He shows in the healing accounts.
- **Atonement:** Trust the Son who appears in God's presence for us and whose saving sacrifice does not need to be repeated.

Leviticus can initially seem to be a book about barriers. Read alongside Hebrews, it can help us recognize God's provision for approaching Him. His holiness is real, and so is His invitation.

## Questions to sit with

> Will we stay outside the tent, or will we step in?

Use the tent as an image of drawing near to God, not as an instruction to bypass requirements for any modern ordinance or sacred space.

- Which part of this lesson helps you trust the Savior more?
- What is one place where you need mercy or help rather than further self-condemnation?
- What could a willing, daily response to His love look like this week?

Allow a private reflection without asking anyone to disclose personal sins or health experiences.

## One response for the week

Choose one action: pray honestly, begin a needed act of repentance, make room for Sabbath worship, offer help to someone overlooked, or return to a small daily practice of discipleship.

When you notice your need for grace, let that need become a reason to approach Christ rather than a reason to stay away. Close with your own testimony of His love and saving power.
