# Closing - Trust the God Who Provides

## Return to the two questions

> "Is any thing too hard for the Lord?"

Genesis 18:14, KJV excerpt.

> "Where is the lamb for a burnt offering?"

Genesis 22:7, KJV excerpt.

[Read Genesis 18:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/18?lang=eng&id=p14#p14)

[Read Genesis 22:7-14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/22?lang=eng&id=p7-p14#p7)

One question confronts the apparent impossibility of a promise. The other asks about something not yet supplied. Both lead our attention toward the Lord rather than toward human control over the outcome.

## Remember the movement

- **Sarah:** A moment of laughter and fear is not the whole measure of her faith. God's faithfulness turns the story toward joy.
- **Abraham's intercession:** Faith can bring serious questions about justice to God and plead for others.
- **Lot's household:** Choices matter, and mercy is also present when people hesitate and need help.
- **Faith for the future:** We can learn from the past without making it a prison for ourselves or others.
- **Abraham and Isaac:** The Lord interrupts harm and provides; Jacob directs our attention toward the Father and His Only Begotten Son.

## Look to Christ for assurance of love

Read Romans 8:32. God's gift of His Son is the foundation for our trust, not our ability to explain everything that happens. Faith need not conceal grief, erase responsibility, or pretend that every desired outcome is guaranteed.

[Read Romans 8:32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/rom/8?lang=eng&id=p32#p32)

## Questions to sit with

- Which passage helps you trust the Lord more honestly today?
- Is there a next step you have been avoiding, or help you need to receive?
- What would it mean to trust Christ's future without denying what the past has cost you?

Give class members room to reflect privately. They need not disclose infertility, trauma, family conflict, or other painful experiences to participate meaningfully.

## One response for the week

Choose one action: pray about an unanswered question, offer help to someone who feels stuck, make a needed change in what receives your attention, or stop using a repented mistake as a permanent verdict.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. We can move toward Him with questions still open, trusting the God who provides.
