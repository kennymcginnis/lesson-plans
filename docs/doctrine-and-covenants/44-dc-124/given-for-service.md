# D&C 124:123-145 - Given for the Work of Ministry

## Find the repeated word

Read verses 123-125, then scan the offices that follow. What word repeatedly introduces the people and offices? Notice the Lord's use of "give" and "given."

[Read D&C 124:123-145](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p123-p145#p123)

The passage presents organization as provision for the community. It names different responsibilities rather than suggesting that one person must carry the whole work.

## What are the offices for?

> "The above offices I have given unto you, and the keys thereof, for helps and for governments, for the work of the ministry and the perfecting of my saints."

D&C 124:143.

The stated purpose is ministry and the Saints' growth. Office is not a measure of personal worth or a guarantee that every decision by an officeholder is beyond question.

Verse 144 invites approval or disapproval of the named officers at conference. Read this alongside the responsibilities rather than treating the people being served as irrelevant to the organization.

## Counsel and cooperation

Earlier counsel warns Almon Babbitt against substituting his own counsel for the appointed Presidency's direction. Other verses describe Hyrum working in concert with Joseph. Coordination and receptiveness matter in a shared work.

[Read D&C 124:84, 91-95](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p84,p91-p95#p84)

Supporting those called to serve can include prayer, honest information, practical help, and respectful questions. It never requires concealing harm or ignoring appropriate accountability.

## Questions to sit with

- What changes when we understand Church service as something given for people's benefit?
- How can we tell whether a responsibility is being used to help rather than elevate the person holding it?
- What can we contribute without needing a prominent assignment?
- What support would help someone fulfill a responsibility well?
