# Closing - Build through Faithful Service

## More than stone and timber

Section 124 calls for buildings, but it also asks for trustworthy people, meek proclamation, care for travelers, ordinances, and a community organized to serve. The work's purpose is people coming to God, not simply projects being completed.

[Read D&C 124:15, 23, 40, 143](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p15,p23,p40,p143#p15)

Remember four themes: strengthen the Saints through integrity, proclaim the gospel with meekness, serve the dead through authorized ordinances, and welcome the weary with genuine care.

## Questions to sit with

- Which responsibility in this revelation feels closest to something you can do now?
- What would integrity look like in that work when no one is watching?
- How can we honor faithful effort without judging only by visible results?

## One response for the week

Keep or repair a commitment, welcome someone new, prepare for worship, work on a family history question, or support a person serving in a demanding role. Choose one realistic action, not a promise to do everything.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. The Lord's work invites our whole-hearted effort, and He knows the circumstances in which we offer it.
