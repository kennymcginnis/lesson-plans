# D&C 124:1-21 - Integrity and Meekness

## God works through people

The opening acknowledges Joseph Smith's offering and prayers and speaks of showing God's wisdom through weak things. The commission that follows is substantial, but its beginning is dependence on God rather than human self-sufficiency.

[Read D&C 124:1-4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p1-p4#p1)

As a personal application, we can take service seriously without expecting to possess every qualification before beginning. Preparation and support remain part of faithfulness.

## A proclamation in the spirit of meekness

Verses 2-4 call for a proclamation to rulers and nations. The message's wide audience does not excuse an arrogant spirit. Verse 4 explicitly requires meekness and the power of the Holy Ghost.

Verse 18 similarly connects preaching with meekness. A message about Christ should be carried in a way that reflects Him. Conviction and humility are not opposites.

## The integrity of the heart

The Lord commends Hyrum Smith:

> "I, the Lord, love him because of the integrity of his heart, and because he loveth that which is right before me."

D&C 124:15, excerpt.

[Read D&C 124:15-21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p15-p21#p15)

George Miller is also described as trustworthy and without guile. His responsibility includes administering blessings to the poor. Integrity is therefore not just a private reputation; it affects whether others can safely entrust resources and responsibilities to us.

Loving what is right includes telling the truth, keeping commitments, acknowledging mistakes, and acting consistently when no one is watching. It does not mean claiming that a trusted person can never fail or should be exempt from accountability.

## Questions to sit with

- Why might meekness be essential when speaking to a powerful or unfamiliar audience?
- What makes integrity more than maintaining a good appearance?
- How does care for the poor give practical meaning to entrusted authority?
- What is one commitment you could keep or repair this week?
