# Build a House unto My Name

Week 44 - Doctrine and Covenants 124

> What kind of people and community are we becoming as we do the Lord's work?

## Lesson notes

Given in Nauvoo on January 19, 1841, section 124 addresses a people rebuilding after displacement from Missouri. Its instructions include a proclamation, individual counsel, a boardinghouse for travelers, a temple, and the organization of Church service.

Study the section through four teaching themes: strengthening the Saints, proclaiming the gospel, redeeming the dead, and welcoming people through the Nauvoo House. These are organizing lenses, not four formal headings in the revelation. Integrity and willing service connect them.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Begin with integrity, 4 minutes:** Read D&C 124:15, 20-21. Ask what makes someone trustworthy in the Lord's work.
2. **Two houses, 7 minutes:** Compare D&C 124:22-24 with 27-31. Distinguish the Nauvoo House from the temple and consider what each provides for people.
3. **Faithful work and real obstacles, 8 minutes:** Read D&C 124:40-41, 49-55. Discuss diligent effort without assuming that every obstruction reveals a failure of faith.
4. **Given for service, 6 minutes:** Read D&C 124:123-125, 143-144. Notice "give" and the purpose of the offices. Choose one response of service or support and close with testimony.

The detailed studies are available for preparation or follow-up. Avoid spending the whole class reconstructing every person's biography or every building instruction.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize D&C 124:4, 15, 20-24, 27-31, 49, and 143-144. Companion passages include verses 40-41, 55, 60-61, and 123-125.

## How the modern teachings connect

The studies use the revelation itself as their foundation. Particular assignments about buildings, property, and offices belong to their historical setting. Our applications concern integrity, hospitality, ordinances, and service rather than a command to repeat every Nauvoo arrangement.

The Nauvoo House was a boardinghouse, not another name for the Nauvoo Temple. Both appear in the revelation, so attend to the purpose of each passage before applying it.
