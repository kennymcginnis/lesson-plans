# Closing - Trust the Redeemer's Reach

## Gather the promises

| Passage | Teaching to remember |
| --- | --- |
| D&C 137:7-9 | The Lord knows works, desires, and opportunities beyond what we can see. |
| D&C 137:10 | Children who die before accountability are saved in the celestial kingdom. |
| D&C 138:1-11 | Pondering scripture and Christ's Atonement can prepare us to receive understanding. |
| D&C 93:33-34; 138:17 | Resurrection reunites spirit and body for a fulness of joy. |
| D&C 138:30-34 | Christ directs the preaching of the gospel among the dead. |
| D&C 138:57-59 | The dead can hear, repent, and receive the blessings of gospel ordinances. |

[Read D&C 137](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/137?lang=eng)

[Read D&C 138:57-60](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/138?lang=eng&id=p57-p60#p57)

The Lord's knowledge is greater than ours, and His saving work extends beyond the limits of mortal opportunity. These revelations invite both hope and participation, without giving us every answer about the experience of a particular loved one.

## Questions to sit with

- Which teaching helps you trust the Savior more deeply?
- How could it change the way you think about someone whose beliefs or opportunities differed from yours?
- What small act of study, remembrance, or service would be a fitting response?

## One response for the week

Ponder a few verses, record a family memory, help with a family history task, or offer quiet support to someone grieving. Do not require a personal loss story in order to participate in the lesson.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. He is the Redeemer of the living and the dead; our role is to receive His grace and serve with hope, not to replace His judgment with our guesses.
