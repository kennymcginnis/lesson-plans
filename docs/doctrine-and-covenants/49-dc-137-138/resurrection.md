# D&C 138:12-24 - A Fulness of Joy

## Joyful, yet awaiting deliverance

Joseph F. Smith sees righteous spirits gathered in hope of resurrection through the grace of the Father and His Son. They rejoice because deliverance is near, yet they still await the reunion of spirit and body.

[Read D&C 138:12-24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/138?lang=eng&id=p12-p24#p12)

Verse 17 describes the body restored and spirit and body united never again to be divided. The hope is more than continued existence after death. It is embodied, enduring life through Christ.

## Why does the body matter?

> "Spirit and element, inseparably connected, receive a fulness of joy."

D&C 93:33, excerpt.

[Read D&C 93:33-34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/93?lang=eng&id=p33-p34#p33)

D&C 138:50 calls the long separation from the body a bondage. This helps explain how righteous spirits can experience joy and still await a greater deliverance.

[Read D&C 138:50-51](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/138?lang=eng&id=p50-p51#p50)

Embodiment is not an obstacle God intends simply to discard. The body belongs in His redemptive purpose. That teaching invites care and respect for bodies now, including those affected by illness, disability, aging, or injury.

## Resurrection and the response to the gospel

First Corinthians 15:20-22 witnesses that all will be made alive in Christ. The resurrection of humanity should be distinguished from receiving every blessing of eternal life regardless of choice. D&C 138 also teaches repentance, ordinances, and judgment according to works.

[Read 1 Corinthians 15:20-22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/15?lang=eng&id=p20-p22#p20)

Keep both truths together: the Savior's victory over death reaches the human family, and our willing response to His gospel matters. Neither truth authorizes us to announce the final judgment of an individual whose heart we do not know.

## Questions to sit with

- Why might the righteous rejoice while still waiting for resurrection?
- How does the promise of embodied joy affect the way we value mortal bodies?
- What is the difference between resurrection and all the blessings associated with eternal life?
- How can resurrection hope support compassion without minimizing grief?
