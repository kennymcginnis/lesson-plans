# Closing - Offer a Willing Heart

## Return to the invitation

> 34. "Behold, the Lord requireth the heart and a willing mind."

D&C 64:34, excerpt.

[Read D&C 64:33-34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/64?lang=eng&id=p33-p34#p33)

The Lord does not ask only for visible activity. He invites a response that reaches our motives, relationships, choices, and willingness to continue learning.

## Revisit the four heart questions

1. Do I look for reasons to condemn, or seek to understand truthfully and charitably?
2. Am I willing to ask the Savior for help in forgiving, while maintaining appropriate safety and accountability?
3. What can I offer willingly to bless others and honor my covenants?
4. What small, sustainable act of well-doing can I continue?

This is a private reflection, not a test with a score. There is no need to reveal financial circumstances, identify someone who caused harm, or describe a personal temptation to the class.

## A heart prepared for a larger work

Section 65 directs our prayers toward the gospel going forth and the world preparing to receive Christ. Section 66 reminds us that this larger work includes personal encouragement, correction, companionship, and continued faithfulness.

We do not need to choose between caring for our own discipleship and helping build Zion. How we speak, forgive, share, and receive counsel shapes the community we are helping to build.

## Questions to sit with

- Which theme is most relevant to you now: forgiveness, building Zion, or personal guidance?
- What is one insight you want to carry into the week?
- What small action would express a willing heart rather than merely a good intention?

## One response for the week

Choose one step: pause before repeating a criticism, pray for help with resentment, offer practical support, keep a small commitment, or seek counsel about a needed change. Include whatever support will make that step realistic.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. The work grows through His power, and our willing response can begin today without pretending that every difficulty has already been resolved.
