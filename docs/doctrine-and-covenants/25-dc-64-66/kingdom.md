# D&C 65 - Prepare Ye the Way of the Lord

## A prayer with a worldwide horizon

Section 65 is short enough to read in full. It calls people to prepare for the Lord, pray, make His works known, and seek the coming of His kingdom. Its vision reaches beyond one congregation or community.

> 2. "The keys of the kingdom of God are committed unto man on the earth, and from thence shall the gospel roll forth unto the ends of the earth."

D&C 65:2, excerpt.

[Read D&C 65](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/65?lang=eng)

Notice the relationship between priesthood keys and the gospel's going forth. The verse does not say that the keys themselves are the stone rolling across the earth. It speaks of authority committed to people and the gospel extending to the nations.

## The stone cut without hands

Verse 2 uses Daniel's image of a stone cut from a mountain without hands. Read Daniel 2:34-35, 44-45 for the dream and its interpretation concerning God's kingdom.

[Read Daniel 2:34-35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/2?lang=eng&id=p34-p35#p34)

[Read Daniel 2:44-45](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/dan/2?lang=eng&id=p44-p45#p44)

**Explicit scriptural connection:** D&C 65 applies Daniel's image to the gospel going forth. The work is God's, not simply the expansion of human influence. Confidence in His purposes should produce humility and service, not contempt for people outside our faith or an ambition to coerce them.

## Russell M. Nelson - Hope of Israel

**Verified quotation; thematic connection.** President Nelson, then President of the Church, describes the gathering of Israel in his June 2018 worldwide youth devotional:

> "That gathering is the most important thing taking place on earth today."

He explains that God's children deserve the opportunity to hear the restored gospel and decide for themselves whether to learn more. Participation includes helping people on both sides of the veil receive gospel blessings, covenants, and ordinances.

["Hope of Israel," June 2018](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/broadcasts/worldwide-devotional-for-young-adults/2018/06/hope-of-israel?lang=eng)

This is a modern teaching on the gathering, not presented here as a direct quotation of D&C 65. It gives a practical way to consider our place in a work larger than ourselves.

## Prepare people to receive Christ

Verses 3-6 speak of preparing for the Bridegroom and praying that the inhabitants of the earth may receive the kingdom. Keep the focus on receiving Christ, not merely counting activity or predicting dates.

Prayer, an invitation offered without pressure, temple and family history service, and a life that makes the gospel's goodness visible can all be responses. Friendship and respect must not depend on whether another person accepts an invitation.

## Questions to sit with

- How does reading this section as a prayer change its tone for you?
- What does the stone's origin suggest about whose work this is?
- How can a worldwide vision give meaning to an ordinary act of service?
- What could you pray for or do this week to help someone receive the Savior's love?
