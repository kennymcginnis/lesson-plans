# The Heart and a Willing Mind

Calendar reference: June 16-22, 2025 - Doctrine and Covenants 64-66

Main reading: Doctrine and Covenants 64-66. The calendar reference identifies the matching study week, not a confirmed teaching date.

> What kind of heart am I willing to let the Lord develop in me?

## Lesson notes

These revelations connect personal discipleship with the building of Zion. The Lord offers mercy, asks His people to forgive, directs their stewardship, and encourages steady work. He also invites prayer for His kingdom and gives William E. McLellin counsel that includes both encouragement and correction.

The thread through the lesson is a willing response to Jesus Christ. Zion is not built only through large undertakings; our judgments, relationships, generosity, and daily faithfulness matter too.

## A private heart exam

Use these questions for reflection, not to score yourself or evaluate someone else:

1. **Do I look for faults, or seek to understand?** Read D&C 64:6, 8, 16. Distinguish charitable judgment from ignoring actual wrongdoing.
2. **Am I willing to seek help in forgiving?** Read D&C 64:1-11. Forgiveness does not require denying harm or abandoning safety and accountability.
3. **Can I offer something to bless others?** Read D&C 64:22-25. Consider time, resources, covenant commitments, and willing service without comparing people's financial circumstances.
4. **Can I continue in well-doing?** Read D&C 64:33-34. Ask what a sustainable next step could be, including needed rest and support.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Begin with the heart, 3 minutes:** Read D&C 64:34 and introduce the four private reflection questions.
2. **Mercy and forgiveness, 8 minutes:** Read D&C 64:6-11, then briefly note verses 12-13. Discuss what forgiveness asks of us and what it does not require.
3. **Small things and a greater work, 7 minutes:** Read D&C 64:33-34 and 65:2, 5-6. Connect one small act of discipleship with preparing people to receive Christ.
4. **Personal guidance and response, 7 minutes:** Read D&C 66:2-3 and 11-13. Ask what it means to receive both encouragement and correction. Allow a quiet response to one heart-exam question and close with your testimony.

The fuller studies are available for preparation or follow-up. Choose one main discussion question per segment rather than attempting every question in the lesson.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize D&C 64:6-11, 33-34; 65:2, 5-6; and 66:2-3, 11-13. Companion readings include D&C 64:12-13, 20-25, 29-32 and Daniel 2:34-35, 44-45.

## How the modern teachings connect

Scripture is the foundation for the discussions. Modern teachings are identified as direct quotations, explicit scripture citations, or thematic connections. Exact words should not be inferred from a summary or a combined reference.

These revelations address particular people and responsibilities in 1831. We can learn from their principles without treating every instruction about property, travel, or missionary companions as a universal command today. The Lord's call for a willing heart is not permission for coercion or an invitation to neglect appropriate care for ourselves and others.
