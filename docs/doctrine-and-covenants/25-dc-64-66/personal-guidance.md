# D&C 66 - Guidance That Encourages and Corrects

## The Lord recognizes real progress

Section 66 addresses William E. McLellin. The opening acknowledges that he has turned from iniquity and received the Lord's truth and everlasting covenant. The next words do not cancel that progress, but they identify more work to do:

> 3. "You are clean, but not all; repent, therefore, of those things which are not pleasing in my sight, saith the Lord, for the Lord will show them unto you."

D&C 66:3, excerpt.

[Read D&C 66:1-4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/66?lang=eng&id=p1-p4#p1)

Encouragement and correction belong in the same relationship. Needing further repentance does not make every previous faithful choice meaningless. Nor does having made progress mean that we no longer need to listen.

## Counsel for an actual person

The revelation includes a missionary assignment, travel directions, a companion, counsel about property, and a warning concerning a particular temptation. The Lord addresses McLellin's life concretely rather than offering only general reassurance.

[Read D&C 66:5-10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/66?lang=eng&id=p5-p10#p5)

Study the instructions in their setting. They do not mean that every disciple should leave home, neglect property, or claim the same healing commission. We can learn to receive personal direction without treating someone else's assignment as a universal command.

Verse 8 includes a companion and the Lord's promise to go with them. Receiving help and instruction is part of the work, not evidence that a calling has failed.

## Thomas S. Monson - Duty Calls

**Verified quotation; thematic connection.** President Monson, then First Counselor in the First Presidency, encourages those who feel shy or inadequate in a calling:

> "Remember that whom the Lord calls, the Lord qualifies."

He explains that the work belongs to the Lord and that His servants can receive His help. This is encouragement to depend on God, not a promise that preparation, training, accommodations, or assistance will become unnecessary.

["Duty Calls," April 1996](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/1996/04/duty-calls?lang=eng)

## Seek guidance without chasing fault in yourself

One prayerful question is, "What would Thou have me change or do next?" Pair it with gratitude for what the Lord has already helped you do. Section 66 begins with both blessing and a call to repent.

Seeking correction is not a demand to discover a hidden sin behind every anxious feeling. Consider scripture, prayer, wise counsel, and the actual facts of your circumstances. Do not treat every intrusive thought as revelation or assume that suffering proves God is displeased.

Choose a concrete, proportionate response: repair a wrong, keep a commitment, ask for help, or prepare for a responsibility. Personal guidance should be consistent with God's commandments and respect others' agency and safety.

## Continue in these things

> 12. "Continue in these things even unto the end, and you shall have a crown of eternal life at the right hand of my Father, who is full of grace and truth."

D&C 66:12.

[Read D&C 66:11-13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/66?lang=eng&id=p11-p13#p11)

The promise is joined to continuing, not merely receiving one meaningful revelation. The section closes by naming the speaker as Jesus Christ, the Redeemer. Our hope rests in Him and His grace, not in a single moment of spiritual certainty.

Verse 11 speaks of bringing many people to Zion with joy. Its word "push" is not a license for coercion. Gospel invitations should respect the very agency through which discipleship becomes willing.

## Questions to sit with

- How do the opening blessing and the correction in verse 3 belong together?
- Why might personal guidance include practical responsibilities as well as spiritual reassurance?
- When has appropriate help made it possible to grow into a responsibility?
- What one change or act of service could you consider privately this week?
