# D&C 93:1-19 - Whom We Worship

## Begin with the purpose

> "I give unto you these sayings that you may understand and know how to worship, and know what you worship, that you may come unto the Father in my name, and in due time receive of his fulness."

D&C 93:19.

[Read D&C 93:1-19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/93?lang=eng&id=p1-p19#p1)

The revelation is not only information about heavenly things. Its purpose is relationship: coming to the Father in the Son's name. Knowing whom we worship should shape trust, prayer, and the kind of life we seek.

## Four reading groups

Read individually or in small groups. Each group identifies one teaching about the Father or Jesus Christ and the words that support it.

| Passage | What to look for | Possible observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D&C 93:1-5 | The invitation and the Father's relationship with the Son | Christ invites repentance, identifies Himself as the true light, and speaks of receiving the Father's fulness. |
| D&C 93:6-10 | John's witness of Christ before and within the world | Christ is the Word, the Redeemer, and the maker of worlds. |
| D&C 93:11-14 | Grace, truth, and growth | The Son did not receive the fulness at first, but continued from grace to grace. |
| D&C 93:15-18 | The baptismal witness and the fulness | The Father bears witness of His Son; John testifies of the Son receiving the Father's glory and power. |

These are starting observations, not a replacement for reading. Ask each group how its discovery could affect worship.

## Read the relationship carefully

Verses 3-5 speak of the Father and Son being one and of the Father's fulness given to the Son. Verses 15-17 also distinguish the Father's voice, the Son, and the Holy Ghost. Do not isolate one expression to erase those distinctions.

The opening promise that faithful souls will see the Lord's face is profound. It does not give a schedule or make a reported vision the measure of another person's faithfulness. Verse 19 includes the phrase "in due time."

## Questions to sit with

- Which title or description of Christ in these verses deepens your trust in Him?
- Why does the Lord connect understanding with worship?
- What changes when worship is understood as coming to the Father through Christ rather than merely attending an event?
- What would you like to learn more fully about the Savior this week?
