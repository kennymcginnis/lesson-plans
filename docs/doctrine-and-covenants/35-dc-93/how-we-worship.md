# D&C 93:19-20 - Worship through Following

## What would it mean to imitate Him?

Ask, "What did Jesus do that I can follow?" Look for His truthfulness, compassion, obedience to the Father, prayer, and service. Worship includes praise and ordinances, but it should also form our character.

[Read D&C 93:19-20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/93?lang=eng&id=p19-p20#p19)

In 3 Nephi, Jesus asks His disciples what manner of people they ought to be and answers:

> 27. "Verily I say unto you, even as I am."

3 Nephi 27:27, excerpt.

[Read 3 Nephi 27:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/27?lang=eng&id=p27#p27)

This is a thematic scriptural companion to D&C 93. Following Christ does not mean reproducing His unique Atonement or assuming His divine authority. It means receiving His grace and letting His example shape our conduct.

## Bruce R. McConkie - Worship and emulation

> "Perfect worship is emulation. We honor those whom we imitate."

This brief statement is attributed to Elder Bruce R. McConkie, of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, in *The Promised Messiah: The First Coming of Christ* (1978), page 568. The original book passage has not been independently checked for these notes. Use it as an attributed teaching, not as a verified quotation from general conference.

The scripture provides the foundation even without the quotation: the Savior invites us to follow Him and come to the Father in His name.

## More than copying an outward action

An act of service can look impressive while seeking recognition; a small kindness can quietly express real love. Ask not only what Christ did, but what His actions show about His love for the Father and for people.

Choose an ordinary setting: a conversation, a disagreement, a responsibility, or an opportunity to help. What response would make our worship visible there? We need not wait for an extraordinary task to practice discipleship.

## Questions to sit with

- What does the word emulation add to your usual understanding of worship?
- Which quality of Jesus Christ could shape one interaction today?
- How do prayer, ordinances, and service support rather than replace one another?
- How can we seek to become like Christ without pretending we can save ourselves?
