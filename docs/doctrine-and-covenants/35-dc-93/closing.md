# Closing - One Step in Light and Truth

## Return to the promise

Read D&C 93:1 and 19-20. The revelation begins with coming to Christ and invites us to come to the Father in His name. Its goal is not only greater knowledge, but a changed relationship and life.

[Read D&C 93:19-20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/93?lang=eng&id=p19-p20#p19)

Remember the movement: know whom you worship, follow the Savior, receive grace, act on light, and share truth through the way you live. Growth does not mean becoming independent of Him.

## Questions to sit with

- What have you learned about Jesus Christ that changes how you want to worship?
- What truth deserves a more consistent response from you?
- What is one thing you can do now, while trusting the Lord to lead you along?

## One response for the week

Choose one small action: act on a teaching, correct a mistaken assumption, make time for a child or neighbor, or resume a sustaining practice of prayer and study. Keep the response private where needed; no one needs to disclose a family conflict or personal sin.

Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ. His promise includes "in due time." We can begin today without demanding that we reach the fulness today.
