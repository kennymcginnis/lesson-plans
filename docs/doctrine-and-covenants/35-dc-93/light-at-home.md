# D&C 93:40-53 - Light and Truth at Home

## Doctrine becomes a responsibility

> "But I have commanded you to bring up your children in light and truth."

D&C 93:40.

[Read D&C 93:40-50](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/93?lang=eng&id=p40-p50#p40)

After teaching about God's glory, eternal identity, and agency, the revelation addresses specific leaders and their homes. Public responsibility does not remove the need for prayer, attention, and repentance in personal relationships.

The counsel is concrete: teach, set things in order, be more diligent and concerned at home, and pray. These are not merely ideas to admire in a class discussion.

## Teaching without controlling

Helping children receive light includes example, honest conversation, kindness, and age-appropriate instruction. It does not mean forcing belief or treating another person's choices as proof that a parent never tried faithfully.

The revelation corrects particular individuals. We should not use it to assign blame for every family's difficulty. Circumstances differ, children have agency, and a supportive community can help rather than shame those carrying demanding responsibilities.

People without children at home also participate in sharing light through friendship, mentoring, teaching, and caring for others. Avoid assuming everyone in the room has the same family situation.

## Keep learning broadly

Verse 53 calls for knowledge of history, countries, kingdoms, and laws as well as scripture translation. The section's interest in light and truth extends beyond a narrow list of religious facts.

[Read D&C 93:51-53](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/93?lang=eng&id=p51-p53#p51)

Learning can serve Zion when it helps us understand people, act responsibly, and contribute wisely. The invitation to spiritual knowledge is not a reason to distrust all secular study or abandon careful evidence.

## Questions to sit with

- Why might the Lord place household counsel beside these teachings about eternity?
- What helps make a home or classroom a place where questions can be asked honestly?
- What is one responsibility that needs more attention rather than another abstract intention?
- What could you learn that would make you better able to serve?
