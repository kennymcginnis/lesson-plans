# Whose I Am, and Whom I Serve

Week 32 - Acts 22-28

> How can a sense of belonging to God shape our response when circumstances are beyond our control?

## Lesson notes

Paul recounts his call, bears witness before authorities, and journeys toward Rome as a prisoner. His ministry includes legal appeals, opposition, a storm, shipwreck, and unexpected hospitality. Faith does not remove practical action or the need for other people's help.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Called to witness, 4 minutes:** Read Acts 22:12-16. Identify the actions Ananias invites Paul to take.
2. **A willing response, 7 minutes:** Read Acts 26:19-29. Discuss commitment without confusing it with flawless performance.
3. **Faith in a storm, 9 minutes:** Read Acts 27:22-25, 31-36. Notice assurance, cooperation, food, and gratitude together.
4. **Continue to serve, 5 minutes:** Read Acts 28:1-10, 30-31. Choose one faithful action and close with testimony.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize Acts 22:13-16; 23:11; 26:28-29; 27:23-25, 31-35; and 28:30-31. Chapters 24-25 place Paul's witness within extended hearings, detention, and appeal to Caesar.

## How the modern teachings connect

The text is the foundation. Unsourced numerical examples about near-perfect performance are not evidence for how God judges disciples. Likewise, the particular circumstances of Paul's voyage are not a general instruction to reject professional expertise or remain in an unsafe situation.
