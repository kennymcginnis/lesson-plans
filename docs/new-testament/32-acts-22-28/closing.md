# Acts 28 - Hospitality and an Open Door

The people of Malta receive the survivors with kindness, warmth, and hospitality. Paul participates in the work and ministers to those who are sick. Their care is not dismissed because they are outside his faith.

[Read Acts 28:1-10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/28?lang=eng&id=p1-p10#p1)

The onlookers change their conclusions about Paul after the viper incident. Their rapid judgments caution us against reading every misfortune as deserved punishment or every escape as proof of personal divinity.

Acts ends in Rome with Paul receiving visitors and teaching about Jesus Christ while under restriction. The book does not close by narrating every later event in his life. Service continues within imperfect circumstances.

[Read Acts 28:23-31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/28?lang=eng&id=p23-p31#p23)

## Questions to sit with

- What can we learn from the islanders' immediate kindness?
- What good could you do within circumstances you cannot fully change?
- How can we avoid assigning a spiritual verdict to another person's misfortune?

Choose an act of hospitality, a truthful witness, or practical support. Close with your own testimony of Christ and the hope of belonging to Him through uncertain journeys.
