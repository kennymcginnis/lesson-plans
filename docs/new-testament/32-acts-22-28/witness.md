# Acts 22-26 - A Witness of What He Has Received

## Brother Saul

Paul recounts Ananias coming to him, calling him brother, and speaking about receiving sight, knowing God's will, and becoming a witness. Read the account's actions before moving to application.

[Read Acts 22:12-21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/22?lang=eng&id=p12-p21#p12)

| In Paul's account | A question for us |
| --- | --- |
| Receive sight and hear God's word | What do I need to learn or see more clearly? |
| Bear witness of what was seen and heard | What can I testify of honestly? |
| Arise, be baptized, and call on the Lord | What response should not be postponed? |
| Be sent to the Gentiles | How could God's work reach beyond my familiar circle? |

These are devotional applications, not a claim that every person receives Paul's apostolic commission. His testimony describes a particular calling.

## Witness and lawful protection

Acts 22-25 includes Paul's assertion of Roman citizenship, hearings, a plot against his life, and an appeal to Caesar. His trust in God does not prevent him from using lawful protections or responding to real danger.

## Almost and altogether

The KJV renders Agrippa's response, "Almost thou persuadest me to be a Christian." Other translations understand his words as a question about persuasion in so short a time. Do not build the whole interpretation on certainty about his private sincerity.

[Read Acts 26:19-29](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/26?lang=eng&id=p19-p29#p19)

Paul's reply expresses a desire for all his hearers to share his faith, but not his chains. As an application, ask where we are holding back a willing response. This is not a formula that 99 percent effort earns 99 percent grace or that every mistake cancels sincere discipleship.

## Questions to sit with

- What stands out about Ananias addressing Paul as brother?
- How can we bear witness without overstating what we know?
- What does Paul's use of legal protection teach us about faith and practical action?
- Where could willingness become one concrete action this week?
