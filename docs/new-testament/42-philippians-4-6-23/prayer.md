# Philippians 4:6-7 - Requests with Thanksgiving

> "In every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God."

Philippians 4:6, KJV excerpt.

[Read Philippians 4:4-7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/philip/4?lang=eng&id=p4-p7#p4)

The KJV phrase "Be careful for nothing" concerns anxious care, not recklessness or neglect of responsibility. Paul invites prayer that includes requests and gratitude. He does not require us to remove every worry before we can speak to God.

## David A. Bednar - Pray Always

**Verified teaching; thematic connection.** Elder Bednar, of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, describes learning of a friend's death. That evening a visiting Apostle, unaware of the loss, suggested that Sister Bednar offer a prayer of thanks without requests.

She expressed gratitude for their friend, the Comforter, the Savior's Atonement and Resurrection, and covenant promises. Elder Bednar describes receiving reassurance and further insight into what they should ask in faith.

["Pray Always," October 2008](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2008/10/pray-always?lang=eng)

> "Prayer becomes more meaningful as we counsel with the Lord in all of our doings, as we express heartfelt gratitude, and as we pray for others."

This reported experience is not a rule that grieving people should offer only thanks or that requests are less spiritual. Philippians explicitly invites requests. The application is to let gratitude deepen prayer, not silence pain.

## Peace that guards

Verse 7 describes God's peace keeping hearts and minds through Christ Jesus. The promise is not that every situation instantly becomes understandable or that anxiety symptoms disappear on command.

Receiving support, seeking treatment, planning responsibly, and praying can belong together. Peace may be experienced amid an unresolved situation. Others may need time and continuing care without being judged as less faithful.

## Questions to sit with

- What do thanksgiving and supplication each contribute to prayer?
- What could you gratefully remember without denying a present concern?
- How might gratitude open a question about how to serve someone else?
- What practical help could accompany the request you are bringing to God?
