# Closing - Gratitude and a Next Step

Paul's invitation joins prayer, thankful remembrance, disciplined attention, action, and dependence on Christ. It also recognizes the people whose gifts make a difference.

[Read Philippians 4:6-9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/philip/4?lang=eng&id=p6-p9#p6)

## Questions to sit with

- What gratitude and what request would you like to bring into the same prayer?
- What is one good thing you can put into practice rather than only admire?
- Who might need a practical expression of care from you?

Reflect privately; no one needs to share a loss or health concern. Choose a small response and close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ, whose strength can sustain us while life remains unfinished.
