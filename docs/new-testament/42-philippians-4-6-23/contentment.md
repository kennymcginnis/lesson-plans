# Philippians 4:8-23 - Practice, Contentment, and Shared Care

## Attend to what is good

Paul names what is true, honest, just, pure, lovely, and of good report. Verse 9 then adds doing what has been learned. Attention and action belong together.

[Read Philippians 4:8-9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/philip/4?lang=eng&id=p8-p9#p8)

This is not permission to ignore injustice, avoid accurate unpleasant information, or blame someone for intrusive thoughts. It invites deliberate attention to what forms a truthful and loving life.

## I have learned

Verses 11-12 describe contentment as something Paul has learned through need and abundance. His declaration of strength follows that account:

> 13. "I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me."

Philippians 4:13, KJV.

[Read Philippians 4:10-13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/philip/4?lang=eng&id=p10-p13#p10)

In context, "all things" concerns his capacity through Christ to live faithfully in different circumstances. It is not a promise that every ambition succeeds, every contest is won, or physical limits cease to exist.

Contentment need not mean passive acceptance of injustice or refusing help. The next verses warmly thank the Philippians for sharing in his affliction.

## Their gift mattered

Read verses 14-19. Paul remembers repeated support, receives their gift, and describes it in the language of an acceptable offering. The closing greetings also place him within a community, including believers in Caesar's household.

[Read Philippians 4:14-23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/philip/4?lang=eng&id=p14-p23#p14)

God's provision and people's generosity are not competing explanations. We can trust Christ and gratefully acknowledge those whose practical care sustains us. Verse 19 is not a guarantee of wealth in exchange for a donation.

## Questions to sit with

- What is the difference between choosing good attention and denying difficult facts?
- Why does Paul's description of need matter to the meaning of verse 13?
- How can contentment coexist with seeking change or accepting help?
- Who has shared a burden with you, and how could you acknowledge it?
