# Closing - Come for Grace

Hebrews invites us to hear the Son, trust the High Priest who understands weakness, and approach God for mercy and help. The point is not to become so impressive that we no longer need the Savior.

[Read Hebrews 4:14-16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/4?lang=eng&id=p14-p16#p14)

## Questions to sit with

- What have these chapters helped you understand about Jesus Christ?
- Which "let us" invitation can shape your week?
- What is one honest request for grace you could make today?

Choose a faithful action and the support needed to take it. Close with your own testimony of Christ, the source of our hope and the One who invites us into God's presence.
