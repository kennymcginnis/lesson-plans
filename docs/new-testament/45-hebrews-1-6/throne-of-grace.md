# Hebrews 3-5 - Rest and the Throne of Grace

## Today, hear His voice

Hebrews 3 recalls the wilderness generation through Psalm 95. Its warning against a hardened heart is directed to the current hearers, not offered as a verdict on an entire people across history.

[Read Hebrews 3:7-19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/3?lang=eng&id=p7-p19#p7)

Chapter 4 develops the promise of God's rest, connecting creation's Sabbath and Israel's story with a rest still offered to God's people. The KJV's "Jesus" in Hebrews 4:8 refers here to Joshua; the names share the same Greek form.

[Read Hebrews 4:1-11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/4?lang=eng&id=p1-p11#p1)

Entering rest is not simply a synonym for avoiding activity. It concerns receiving what God offers through faithful trust. Rest and the invitation to labor toward it should not become contradictory demands for endless productivity.

## Help in time of need

> 16. "Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need."

Hebrews 4:16, KJV.

[Read Hebrews 4:14-16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/4?lang=eng&id=p14-p16#p14)

The preceding verse describes a High Priest touched by our infirmities, tested yet without sin. Boldness is confidence in Him, not a claim that we no longer need mercy. Weakness becomes a reason to come, not a reason to remain outside until we are self-sufficient.

## He learned obedience

Hebrews 5:7-9 speaks of prayers, tears, obedience, suffering, and Christ as the source of eternal salvation. "Made perfect" should not be read as saying Jesus needed to repent of moral wrongdoing. The passage concerns the completion of His saving vocation through real experience.

[Read Hebrews 5:1-10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/5?lang=eng&id=p1-p10#p1)

Do not turn His suffering into a claim that every harm we experience was deliberately assigned to improve us. He can meet and redeem suffering without our knowing its cause. Appropriate safety, support, and healing remain important.

## Questions to sit with

- What makes hearing "today" different from postponing every response?
- How does the Savior's understanding affect the way you hear "come boldly"?
- What is the difference between confidence in grace and confidence in our own perfection?
- What help do you need to receive rather than earn alone?
