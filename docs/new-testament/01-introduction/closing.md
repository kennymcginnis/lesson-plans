# Closing - Learn and Respond

> 29. "Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me."

Matthew 11:29, KJV excerpt.

[Read Matthew 11:28-30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/11?lang=eng&id=p28-p30#p28)

The year begins with an invitation, not a demand to understand everything immediately. We can study responsibly, listen to the Gospel witnesses, and practice what the Savior teaches.

## Questions to sit with

- What do you hope to learn about Jesus Christ?
- What question will you bring to your reading?
- Therefore, what? What is one action that could make this learning part of your life?

Choose a modest study plan and one practical response. Close with your own testimony, leaving room for others to speak honestly about what they know and what they are still learning.
