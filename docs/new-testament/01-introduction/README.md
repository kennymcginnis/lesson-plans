# Learn of Me

Week 1 - New Testament Introduction

> What will learning about Jesus Christ invite us to become?

## Lesson notes

Begin with responsibility for our own learning, then consider the world of the New Testament and the distinctive voices of the four Gospels. Their purpose is not exhausted by collecting information. We read to know the Savior and respond to Him.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Begin with the invitation, 4 minutes:** Read Matthew 11:28-30 and ask what it means to learn of Christ.
2. **Learning that changes us, 7 minutes:** Use the four learning approaches and the question "Therefore, what?" Choose one practical response.
3. **Hear four voices, 8 minutes:** Compare the openings of Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John. Notice what each emphasizes without forcing them into identical accounts.
4. **Read with care, 6 minutes:** Introduce the Jewish and Roman setting, name one assumption to avoid, and choose a sustainable study practice. Close with testimony.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize Matthew 11:28-30; Matthew 1:1; Mark 1:1; Luke 1:1-4; and John 1:1-5. The historical overview provides context, not a substitute for the Gospel accounts.

## How the modern teachings connect

Distinguish a helpful teaching illustration from a claim about history or authorship. The Gospels are witnesses to Jesus Christ within a Jewish setting, not grounds for stereotypes about Jewish people. Modern quotations are used only with their source and verification status made clear.
