# Closing - Seek His Will

Jesus makes room for communion, resists demands to misuse His power, and begins a ministry of good news. Those who follow Him are invited into that work.

[Read Luke 5:16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/5?lang=eng&id=p16#p16)

## Questions to sit with

- What could help you turn toward God before reacting to a pressure?
- Which teaching of Jesus would you like to remember in a difficult moment?
- What is one practical act of preparation or service you can choose now?

Choose one manageable response, including appropriate rest or support. Close with your own testimony of Christ. We follow Him through grace and willing discipleship, not by proving that we never need help.
