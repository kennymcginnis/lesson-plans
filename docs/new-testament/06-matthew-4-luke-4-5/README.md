# The Spirit of the Lord Is upon Me

Week 6 - Matthew 4; Luke 4-5

> How can we seek the Father's will instead of demanding that He prove Himself on our terms?

## Lesson notes

Jesus withdraws into the wilderness, resists temptation, announces His ministry, and calls disciples. The lesson connects quiet preparation with faithful action. Scripture strengthens His response, but the temptation account also shows how a scripture can be misused.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Make room for God, 4 minutes:** Read Luke 4:1-2 and 5:16. Consider prayerful preparation without treating exhaustion or hunger as moral failure.
2. **Three temptations, 9 minutes:** Read Matthew 4:3-10. Use the comparison table to identify each invitation and Jesus' reply.
3. **The mission and the call, 7 minutes:** Read Luke 4:16-21 and 5:1-11. Connect resisting temptation with the work Jesus came to do.
4. **A faithful response, 5 minutes:** Choose a sustaining passage and a practical response, then close with testimony.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize Matthew 4:1-11; Luke 4:16-21; and Luke 5:1-11, 16. Jesus' replies draw on Deuteronomy 8:3; 6:16; and 6:13.
