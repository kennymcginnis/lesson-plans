# Closing - Love in Deed and Truth

God's love comes first. The letters invite us to receive it through Christ, walk truthfully, repent, and care for people in concrete ways. Confidence grows from abiding in Him, not comparing ourselves with an imagined inferior group.

[Read 1 John 3:16-18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-jn/3?lang=eng&id=p16-p18#p16)

## Questions to sit with

- What would make your love more visible in action this week?
- Where do you need mercy, or help in showing it?
- How could a truthful response also be a compassionate one?

Choose one act of care, one repair, or one prayerful return to Christ. Keep sensitive matters private. Close with your own testimony of His love and the hope of life in Him.
