# Love in Deed and Truth

Week 49 - 1-3 John; Jude

> How does receiving God's love change what we believe, how we act, and whom we care for?

## Lesson notes

The letters connect witness of Jesus Christ with walking in light, keeping commandments, and loving people in practical ways. They also address discernment, hospitality, harmful leadership, and faithfulness amid misleading teaching.

The main activity follows 1 John's contrasts and promises. Read its strong language as an invitation to examine our own response, not a system for dividing everyone we know into worthy insiders and worthless outsiders.

## A 25-minute class

1. **God's love comes first, 4 minutes:** Read 1 John 1:1-4 and 4:9-10, 19. Begin with Christ, not a scorecard of performance.
2. **Love made visible, 8 minutes:** Use three rows of the comparison activity, including 1 John 3:16-18. Ask what an actual response would look like.
3. **Abide with confidence, 7 minutes:** Read 1 John 1:8-9, 2:1-2, and 5:14-15. Distinguish assurance in Christ from a claim to sinlessness or guaranteed outcomes.
4. **Truth and hospitality, 6 minutes:** Compare 3 John 5-11 with Jude 20-23, choose one act of faithful care, and close with testimony.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize 1 John 1:1-9; 2:1-6; 3:16-18; 4:7-21; and 5:11-15. Read 2 John, 3 John, and Jude in full as short letters with distinct concerns.

## How the modern teachings connect

The studies use the letters' own words and distinguish the KJV from JST readings. The question "What would a holy young adult do?" is attributed to Wendy W. Nelson's "One Question That Can Change Your Life"; its original publication has not been independently checked here, so it is not presented as a verified quotation. The practical reflection rests on the letters' teaching about Christlike love.
