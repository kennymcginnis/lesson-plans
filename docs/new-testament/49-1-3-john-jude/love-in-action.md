# 1 John 2-5 - How Do I Show Love?

## Begin with His love

> 19. "We love him, because he first loved us."

1 John 4:19, KJV.

[Read 1 John 4:7-21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-jn/4?lang=eng&id=p7-p21#p7)

God's sending of His Son precedes our response. Love and obedience do not purchase His initial concern for us. They express receiving and living in the love He has shown.

## A comparison activity

Read a pair of passages, identify the contrast, and describe one action consistent with Christ. Use the table to examine choices rather than assign labels to people.

| Theme | Response toward Christ | Contrasting warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowing God | Keep His word; walk as Jesus walked (2:3-6). | Claim to know Him while refusing His commands (2:4). |
| Love of neighbor | Abide in light and love one another (2:10; 4:7-8, 11, 21). | Claim light while hating another (2:9-11; 4:20). |
| Practical care | Give and serve in deed and truth (3:16-18). | Close compassion to someone in need (3:17). |
| Witness of Christ | Confess Jesus Christ come in the flesh and the Son of God (4:2, 15; 5:1, 5). | Deny the Father and Son or reject Christ's coming in the flesh (2:22-23; 4:3). |
| Priorities | Do God's will (2:17). | Give life over to disordered desires and pride (2:15-16). |

[Read 1 John 2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-jn/2?lang=eng)

[Read 1 John 3:10-18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-jn/3?lang=eng&id=p10-p18#p10)

The letter's contrast between children of God and children of the devil concerns conduct and allegiance. It is not a claim that Church membership by itself proves love or that those outside the Church lack worth. The phrase "love not the world" does not cancel the duty to love people.

## Not words alone

> 18. "Let us not love in word, neither in tongue; but in deed and in truth."

1 John 3:18, KJV excerpt.

The invitation does not make kind speech unimportant. It refuses a verbal claim of love that withholds practical compassion. Food, time, listening, advocacy, and honest care can give substance to what we profess.

Sacrificial love is not permission to demand unsafe self-neglect from someone else. Respecting boundaries, telling the truth about harm, and obtaining appropriate support can all express love.

## Questions to sit with

- Which contrast most clearly challenges a habit rather than just an opinion?
- Who might need practical help instead of another general expression of concern?
- How can we be truthful about our convictions without hatred toward someone who differs?
- What would a holy and loving response look like in one ordinary interaction today?
