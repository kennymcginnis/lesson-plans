# 1 John 1-2 - The Word of Life

## Witness becomes an invitation to fellowship

First John begins with what has been heard, seen, and handled concerning the Word of life. Compare its opening with John 1:1-18: both speak of the beginning and life manifested through Christ, though the letter is not another continuous Gospel narrative.

[Read 1 John 1:1-4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-jn/1?lang=eng&id=p1-p4#p1)

[Read John 1:1-18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=p1-p18#p1)

The witness is shared so readers may have fellowship with the writers, the Father, and His Son. Knowledge of Christ is not presented as a private badge of superiority. Its aim includes shared life and joy.

## God is light

Verses 5-7 connect God's light with truthfulness and walking in fellowship. The claim to know Him is tested by how people live, not only by what they say.

Read the qualification immediately afterward: claiming to have no sin is self-deception. Confession and cleansing belong to walking in the light.

[Read 1 John 1:5-10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-jn/1?lang=eng&id=p5-p10#p5)

## An advocate with the Father

First John 2:1-2 names Jesus Christ as our advocate and speaks of His atoning work for the sins of the whole world. The letter seeks to turn people from sin while giving them a reason to return when they fail.

[Read 1 John 2:1-6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-jn/2?lang=eng&id=p1-p6#p1)

Obedience is an expression of knowing Him; it is not proof that we have stopped needing grace. Read later statements about those born of God not sinning alongside this acknowledgment of confession and advocacy. They address the incompatibility of a life committed to sin with life in Christ, not a test that condemns every repentant person after a mistake. The JST's rendering of 1 John 3:9 is a distinct textual interpretation, not the KJV wording.

## Questions to sit with

- What does fellowship add to the purpose of bearing witness?
- Why must we read the promise of light beside the warning against claiming sinlessness?
- How does Christ's advocacy affect the way we approach repentance?
- What honest acknowledgment or change would help you walk more openly in His light?
