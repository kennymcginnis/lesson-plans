# 2-3 John; Jude - Truth, Welcome, and Perseverance

## Love and discernment in 2 John

The elder joins truth, love, and walking in the commandments, then warns about teachers who deny Jesus Christ coming in the flesh. Its restrictions on receiving such a teacher concern support for a mission judged deceptive; they should not become a ban on ordinary kindness toward people with different beliefs.

[Read 2 John](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/2-jn/1?lang=eng)

Receiving a person with dignity does not require endorsing every teaching. Discernment and love belong together, not on opposite sides of a choice.

## Hospitality and status in 3 John

Third John commends Gaius for supporting traveling believers and criticizes Diotrephes for seeking preeminence, speaking maliciously, and obstructing hospitality. The issue is not only doctrinal words but the use of influence within the community.

[Read 3 John](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/3-jn/1?lang=eng)

Ask whether our responsibilities help others serve or protect our own importance. Verse 11's invitation is to follow what is good. The closing desire to speak face to face also reminds us that written messages are not the whole of relationship.

## Jude: keep yourselves in God's love

Jude warns against people who turn grace into permission for wrongdoing. Its strong examples draw on scriptural and other Jewish traditions, including material associated with Enoch. We need not settle every historical question about those traditions before recognizing the practical closing invitations.

[Read Jude 17-25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/jude/1?lang=eng&id=p17-p25#p17)

Build on faith, pray in the Holy Ghost, remain in God's love, look for Christ's mercy, and show compassion. Contending for faith is not an instruction to be contentious or cruel. The final praise rests in God's ability to keep and present His people, not merely their own vigilance.

## Questions to sit with

- How can we distinguish supporting a harmful message from treating a person humanely?
- What does Diotrephes' behavior reveal about the misuse of religious influence?
- Which closing invitation in Jude can you practice now?
- What would truthful, discerning hospitality look like in our community?
