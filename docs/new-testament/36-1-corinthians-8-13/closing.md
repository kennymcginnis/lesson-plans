# Closing - Use Your Gifts for Love

Paul's concerns belong together: freedom should build others up, temptation calls for humility and help, worship should not humiliate the poor, and gifts find their purpose in love.

[Read 1 Corinthians 13:13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/13?lang=eng&id=p13#p13)

## Questions to sit with

- What would your use of time, freedom, and abilities tell someone about your priorities?
- Whose contribution could you recognize more fully?
- What is one action that would turn admiration for charity into its practice?

Offer a sincere acknowledgment, ask for help with a weakness, or use a strength for someone else's good. Close with your own testimony of Jesus Christ, whose love gives these practices their direction.
