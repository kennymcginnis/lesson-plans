# Gifts Given for Love

Week 36 - 1 Corinthians 8-13

> What would someone learn about our priorities by seeing how we use our freedom and gifts?

## Lesson notes

Paul joins questions of rights, conscience, temptation, worship, and spiritual gifts with concern for the community. Freedom is not only permission to do what we prefer. Gifts are not medals of superiority. Love gives Christian service its purpose.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Examine our priorities, 4 minutes:** Read 1 Corinthians 8:1 and 9:19-23. Ask how love shapes freedom.
2. **Choose a way through, 7 minutes:** Read 1 Corinthians 10:12-14. Discuss the promised way of escape without treating every hardship as something we must bear alone.
3. **Gifts for the common good, 8 minutes:** Read 1 Corinthians 12:7, 14-26. Use the gifts reflection and identify whose contribution is easily overlooked.
4. **The more excellent way, 6 minutes:** Read 1 Corinthians 13:4-7. Choose one expression of love and close with testimony.

## Scriptures to emphasize

Prioritize 1 Corinthians 8:1, 9-13; 9:19-27; 10:13; 11:23-29; 12:7, 14-26; and 13:1-13.

## How the modern teachings connect

The common-good teaching is 1 Corinthians 12:7. The studies use Paul's actual argument before making personal applications; his discussion of temple workers in chapter 9 concerns support for ministry, not a description of modern temple ordinances. Personality descriptions can prompt reflection but are not identical to the spiritual gifts Paul names.
