# Come to the Savior

Week 10 - Matthew 8; Mark 2-4; Luke 7

> What do these encounters teach us about the Savior's power and willingness to care?

## Lesson notes

The lesson follows people who come to Jesus with illness, loss, fear, and need for forgiveness. Sometimes another person helps them reach Him. The accounts invite us to receive His compassion and participate in caring for others.

## A 25-minute class

1. **Bring someone to Christ, 5 minutes:** Read Mark 2:1-12. Notice the friends' effort and Jesus' response.
2. **Receive His compassion, 8 minutes:** Choose Matthew 8:1-4 or Luke 7:11-17. Distinguish the reported miracle from a guarantee about every person's timetable.
3. **Trust amid the storm, 7 minutes:** Read Mark 4:35-41. Ask what the disciples fear and what they learn about Jesus.
4. **Respond with care, 5 minutes:** Read Matthew 8:14-15, choose one act of service or request for help, and close with testimony.

## Scriptures to emphasize

The encounter guide preserves seven accounts. Also read Mark 2's teaching about fasting and the Sabbath, Mark 3's conflicts and calling of the Twelve, Mark 4's seed parables, and Luke 7's discussions of John the Baptist and the woman at Simon's house. These chapters are more than a list of miracles.

## How the modern teachings connect

James W. McConkie III's October 2022 teaching on Mark 2 was checked against its published text. The miracle accounts witness of Christ; they do not authorize us to blame continuing illness on insufficient faith. Ritual status, moral guilt, disability, and human worth are not interchangeable categories.
