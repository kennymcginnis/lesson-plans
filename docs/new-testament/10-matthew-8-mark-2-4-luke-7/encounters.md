# Seven Encounters with the Savior

## Read an encounter closely

Choose an account. Identify who comes, who helps, what Jesus does or says, and how people respond. Then consider a personal application without inventing details about the participants' feelings.

| Encounter | Passage | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A man lowered through a roof | Mark 2:1-12 | What does Jesus see in the friends' effort? |
| A man seeking cleansing | Matthew 8:1-4 | What does Jesus' touch communicate? |
| A centurion's servant | Matthew 8:5-13; Luke 7:1-10 | How does the officer understand authority? |
| The widow of Nain | Luke 7:11-17 | What prompts Jesus' response? |
| Peter's mother-in-law | Matthew 8:14-15 | What follows her healing? |
| The storm | Matthew 8:23-27; Mark 4:35-41 | What question do the disciples ask about Jesus? |
| Deliverance from demons | Matthew 8:28-34 | How do people respond to His authority? |

## Friends who make a way

The crowd prevents the four from reaching Jesus with the man they carry. They open the roof and lower him. Jesus sees their faith and addresses the man with forgiveness before commanding him to rise.

**Verified teaching on Mark 2:** Elder James W. McConkie III, of the Seventy, identifies Christ's power to forgive, heal, and recognize the intentions of those who help others come to Him.

> "He sees our true intentions and will appropriately honor them."

[October 2022 general conference address](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2022/10/26mcconkie?lang=eng)

Helping someone can include removing a practical barrier, listening, arranging access, or offering companionship. Respect their agency and wishes. The friends' unusual action is not a general instruction to damage property or override boundaries.

## Questions to sit with

- Which encounter helps you understand something you need from Christ?
- What prevents people from receiving support in our community?
- How can we help without making another person a project?
- What does Christ's authority to forgive add to this healing account?
