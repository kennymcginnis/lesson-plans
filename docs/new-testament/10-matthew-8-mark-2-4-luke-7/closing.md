# Closing - Receive and Reflect His Care

The accounts show Jesus forgiving, healing, noticing grief, and meeting fear. We can come as people who need Him and help others without pretending we control the form or timing of a miracle.

[Read Matthew 8:14-17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/8?lang=eng&id=p14-p17#p14)

## Questions to sit with

- Which encounter helps you trust the Savior more deeply?
- What kind of help do you need to receive rather than keep managing alone?
- Who could experience a little of His care through an action you take?

Choose a respectful act of support or a request for help. Close with your own testimony of Christ. Continuing illness or grief is not evidence that a person has failed this lesson's invitation.
